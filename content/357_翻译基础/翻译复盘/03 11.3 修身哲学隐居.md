# 11.3 修身哲学隐居

> 由 PDF 转 Markdown 生成，保留原文 / 译文 / 批注。`~~删除线~~` = 修订痕迹；`<u>下划线</u>` = 重点标注；引用块 = 英文段落。

大学毕业后由于我痴迷老庄、陶渊明的修身哲学，我决意模仿中国古代圣贤的生活方式，隐居到苏格兰深山乡野里，<u>自己辟了一块荒地躬耕自食</u>。我知道两千年前我所景仰的陶兄曾如此这般地融入大自然。

> Upon graduation, I decided to live in the manner of ancient Chinese sages, because I was fascinated by the character-building philosophy of Laozi, Zhuangzi and Tao Yuanming. In a remote village in Scotland, I <u>cleared land for farming</u> and lived as a hermit. I knew that Tao Yuanming, whom I admire, was living this way to <u>commune with nature</u>.

> 用：emulate/ imitate the lifestyle of ancient Chinese sages 也 ok

修身哲学: the self-cultivation philosophy

我一边<u>务农</u>、一边研读中国古代哲学、元曲及明清小说，并为<u>报刊</u>撰稿<u>赚取生活费</u>。编辑们对我的英文风格深感惊讶和新鲜，报社来信赞美我“难以想象，如<u>天使般的行文</u>”。

> While tending the fields, I delved into ancient Chinese philosophy, the scripts of operas from the Yuan Dynasty, and novels from the Ming and Qing dynasties. I also wrote articles for newspapers to supplement my income. Stunned by my refreshing articles, the editors <u>wrote me letters</u> in praise of my “unimaginable, <u>angelic style</u>”.

我深知那是因为“启承转合”的中文写作玄机启发了我的思维，璀璨的中国文化给予了我享用不尽的灵感。直至今日，我仍坚持用中国文学的技巧逻辑来下笔英文散文。

> Deep inside, however, I know that it owes much to the skills that I have learned from Chinese writing: introduction, elucidation, transition and conclusion, and to the inexhaustible inspiration that the splendid Chinese culture offers. Even today, I continue to draw upon skills and logic of Chinese literature in my English writing.

起承转合：指文章结构的四个组成部分（起：开头；承：承接上文；转：转折；合：结束）乘：elucidation!!!!!!!!!!!!!

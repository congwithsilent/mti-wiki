## Part 2：逐句纠错

### 第一段

**S1｜跨文化传播实践中，"翻译即背离"是一个难以回避的痛点。**
- **问题：** 术语错（diffusion）、程度错（inevitable）、意思错（disadvantage）。"there is…, which is that…"句式绕了两层，阅卷时显得冗长。"翻译即背离"呼应的是意大利谚语 *traduttore, traditore*，有现成的英文说法。
- **A：** In cross-cultural communication, "translation is betrayal" is a problem that is hard to avoid.
- **B：** In cross-cultural communication, the notion that "to translate is to betray" remains a persistent pain point that can hardly be sidestepped.

**S2｜将中国的古诗词翻译成英文……时常面临误读。**
- **问题：** 两个分句都用 Translating… results in，后一句还漏了"转化为国际话语"。"意境""理念"两个关键词都译错了。
- **A：** When classical Chinese poetry is translated into English, its artistic conception is often almost entirely lost; when China's development philosophy is put into international discourse, it is often misinterpreted.
- **B：** Rendered into English, classical Chinese poetry often loses virtually all of its artistic conception; recast in international discourse, China's development philosophy is frequently misread.
  （B 句两个过去分词的逻辑主语正好是后面的主句主语，不构成悬垂结构。）

**S3｜这是因为语言之外，有很多不言而喻，甚至觉察不到的知识。**
- **问题：** reason of 搭配错；above 译错"之外"；which…it 是冗余复指；"觉察不到"被改写成"被忽视"。这一句有四处硬伤。
- **A：** This is because, beyond language, there is a great deal of knowledge that is taken for granted or even imperceptible.
- **B：** This is because beyond language lies a vast body of knowledge that is self-evident, or even imperceptible.
  （B 句用了地点状语前置的全倒装。）

**S4｜中国要全面提升国际话语权……"诉说"自己。**
- **问题：** 主语"中国"被换成 we，丢了 China's；"了解"被译成 learn from；"诉说"被译成 interpret；then 前缺连词。
- **A：** To fully enhance its international discourse power, China must first understand others and then tell its own story without compromising its principles and position.
- **B：** For China to comprehensively strengthen its voice in international discourse, it must first come to understand others, and only then "speak" for itself without compromising its principled position.

### 第二段

**S5｜讲好中国故事，传播好中国声音……搭建桥梁。**
- **问题：** 口号只译了一半，还自创了 vividly。The key point of how to… is to… 结构臃肿。"之间"地带漏译。
- **A：** The key to telling China's stories well and making China's voice heard lies in crossing the "gap" between Chinese and Western cultures and building bridges in this "in-between" space.
- **B：** Telling China's stories well and making China's voice heard hinges on how we traverse the "gap" between Chinese and Western cultures and build bridges within this "in-between" space.

**S6｜这要求超越简单的语言转换，进行意义的重构。**
- **问题：** transformation of languages 意思错；step up from 是中式表达。
- **A：** This requires going beyond simple linguistic conversion to reconstruct meaning.
- **B：** This calls for moving beyond mere linguistic transfer toward the reconstruction of meaning.

**S7｜我们需要寻找不同文化的契合点，才能使种种文化彼此滋润而互相丰富。**
- **问题：** "才能"的条件关系被 for 弱化了；"相互"的意思丢了；several 是误译。
- **A：** We need to find common ground between different cultures so that they can nourish and enrich each other.
- **B：** Only by identifying points of convergence among different cultures can we enable them to nourish and enrich one another.
  （B 句用 Only by… 倒装，正好对应"才能"。）

### 第三段

**S8｜具体而言，我们要将中国理念与世界各国的共同关切相对接。**
- **问题：** theory 是重复错误；concern 单复数错；"共同"漏译。
- **A：** Specifically, we should align Chinese concepts with the shared concerns of countries around the world.
- **B：** Specifically, we must dovetail Chinese ideas with the common concerns of countries worldwide.

**S9｜例如，在阐述"构建人类命运共同体"时……公共产品属性。**
- **问题：** when we introducing 是语法硬伤；官方术语语序错；public product 是术语错误。这是全文政治分量最重的一句，三处错误放在一起，阅卷时容易直接降档。
- **A：** For example, when explaining the vision of "building a community with a shared future for mankind", we should emphasize its nature as a public good for global governance.
- **B：** When elaborating on "building a community with a shared future for mankind", for instance, we should highlight its attribute as a public good in global governance.

**S10｜既要善于运用……但同时要保留中国文化的核心神韵。**
- **问题：** Not only 后没有倒装；but also 后缺主语；goals 和 meaning 两处意思错；"善于"漏译。另外原文"既要……但同时要"本身就是关联词混用，译文不必照搬，选 while 或 Not only…but also 中的一种即可。
- **A：** We should be good at using internationally accepted concepts and logic to explain China's propositions, while preserving the core essence of Chinese culture.
- **B：** Not only must we be adept at explaining China's propositions through internationally accepted concepts and logic, but we must also preserve the essential spirit of Chinese culture.

**S11｜这需要传播者具备深厚的双语双文化能力……"隐性知识"。**
- **问题：** demand sb to do 语法错；translators 把主体缩小了；classify that which is 句法混乱；两个学术术语都没译出来。
- **A：** This requires communicators to have strong bilingual and bicultural competence, so that they can tell which knowledge is "explicit knowledge" that can be conveyed through language and which is "tacit knowledge" that can only be perceived through contextual experience.
- **B：** This demands that communicators possess deep bilingual and bicultural competence, enabling them to distinguish "explicit knowledge", which can be conveyed through language, from "tacit knowledge", which can only be grasped through situated experience.

### 第四段

**S12–13｜对于隐性知识……借助个体命运、生活细节来表述。**
- **问题：** 照搬中文的话题结构，加了冗余的 it；while 误用成对比；"命运"漏译；介词 in 错。原文两句是"总述—展开"的关系，合并时应该用冒号，或者干脆分成两句。
- **A：** Tacit knowledge can be transformed through narrative. Abstract cultural concepts need to be expressed through individual destinies and details of daily life.
- **B：** Tacit knowledge lends itself to narrative transformation: abstract cultural concepts are best articulated through individual destinies and the textures of everyday life.

**S14｜全球受众，尤其是年轻一代，更倾向于通过具体的故事来感知文化。**
- **问题：** feel the culture 是中式表达；detailed 破坏了与上文 abstract 的对照；"更倾向于"的比较意味弱化了。
- **A：** Global audiences, especially younger generations, prefer to perceive culture through concrete stories.
- **B：** Global audiences, particularly the younger generation, are more inclined to experience culture through concrete stories.

**S15｜因此，我们需要从"国家视角"适度下沉到"个体视角"……**
- **问题：** So 是口语语体；properly 意思错；sink down 和 dig up 是中式动词；缺冠词。一句有五处错误。
- **A：** Therefore, we need to shift moderately from a "national perspective" to an "individual perspective" and find stories that embody the common values of humanity.
- **B：** We therefore need to shift, in measured fashion, from a "national perspective" to an "individual perspective", unearthing stories that embody the common values of humanity.

**S16｜通过展现中国人民对家庭的眷恋……激起"同理心"。**
- **问题：** Through doing 用错，应为 By doing；三个名词短语 reliance of、preservance、fighting 全错；sympathy 不等于 empathy。原文"通过……能够……"没有主语，翻译时必须补一个主语（we），或者把动名词短语直接作主语，绝不能写成"Through showing… can evoke"这种没有主语的句子。
- **A：** By showing the Chinese people's attachment to family, perseverance in striving, and longing for peace, we can evoke empathy among foreign audiences.
- **B：** Portraying the Chinese people's deep attachment to family, their unwavering commitment to hard work and their aspiration for peace can strike a chord of empathy with audiences abroad.

**S17｜这种基于人性共通点的"情感共振"……理解中国道路背后的逻辑。**
- **问题：** 用 while 硬连成流水句；核心概念"情感共振"漏译；digest 意思接近反了；have willingness 主语错位；receive the ideology 歪曲了政治含义。这是全文错误最集中的一句，也是政治风险最高的一句。
- **A：** This "emotional resonance", which is based on shared human nature, can effectively dispel ideological prejudice and make audiences more willing to understand the logic behind China's path with an open mind.
- **B：** Rooted in our shared humanity, such "emotional resonance" can effectively dispel ideological bias, leaving audiences more willing to approach the logic underpinning China's path with an open mind.

## 冲 80+：三个最值得先改的地方

1. **建一张官方术语表。** 本文丢分最多的是理念、命运共同体、讲好中国故事、公共产品、显性/隐性知识。备考时把 CGTN 和外交部的高频表述整理成一张表，反复背。
2. **长难句先拆成短句。** 本文 7 处 Logic 扣分中有 3 处来自误用 while 连句。考场上一句话只表达一层意思，用分号或句号断开，比硬连安全。
3. **动词先过一遍"英文里真有这种说法吗"。** sink down、dig up、digest、feel the culture 都是把中文动词逐字搬过来。每次写完一个动词就检查一次，大概能挽回 15 分左右。

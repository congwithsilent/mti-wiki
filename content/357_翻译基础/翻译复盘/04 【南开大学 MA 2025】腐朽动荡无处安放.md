> ~~On~~ the ~~shadowy~~ sky there is a ~~big~~, moist and heavy cloud that may bring about rain. It often occurs to me that whether it can be compared to the relationship between Lu Xun and the times he ~~belongs~~ belonged to.

> In the gloomy sky there gathered a dense, towering cloud that foretold the arrival of heavy rain. I often wonder whether it can be compared to the relationship between Lu Xun and the era he lived in.

那是腐朽到了糜烂程度而又极其动荡不安的时代。

> That was an era of great corruption and social turbulence.

> That was an era of despairing corruption and relentless upheaval.

鲁迅企盼着有什么力量能一举劈开那阴霾，带给他自己也带给世人，尤其中国底层民众，又尤其许许多多迷惘、彷徨，被人生的无助和民族的不振所困扰，连呐喊几声都将招至凶视的青年以光明和希望。

> Lu Xun looked to a kind of force that could cleave the shadow and bring light as well as hope to not only himself but also the general populace, in particular, the Chinese grassroots, and numerous and ~~indecisive~~ young peoples beset by ~~their helpless lives and sluggish nation~~ where several ~~clamors~~ could lead to ~~ordeals~~.

clamor v.大声要求，愤怒疾呼 n.嘈杂声，吵闹声（显然不合适被人生的无助和民族的不振所困扰，主要强调的是无助、不振，因此要把它们放前面。凶视即怒视

> Lu Xun longed for a certain force that could cleave through the dark cloud and bring light and hope to himself, to other people, and especially to the lower classes in his country and to young people who were confused, burdened by <u>the sense of helplessness</u> and disheartened by their <u>nation’s stagnation</u>, and who were met with malicious eyes when they cried out for change.

然而他敏锐的，善于深刻洞察的眼所见，除了腐朽和动荡不安，还是腐朽和动荡不安，更不可救药的腐朽和更鸡飞狗跳的动荡不安。

> What his acute and deeply observable eyes saw is nothing but the corruption and social turbulence.

> However, all that his sharp eyes, which showed great perception, saw was corruption and upheaval - nothing but corruption and upheaval, or rather, incurable corruption and upheaval spiraling into utter chaos.（陷入完全混乱）

# 11.20 流逝

1.在我的<u>记忆的深处</u>，<u>珍藏</u>着一些难忘的<u>画面</u>。当我<u>翻拣</u>出它们时，种种难以用文字描述的感慨常<u>拨动我的心弦</u>。

> Deep in my ~~memory~~ (mind), there are some indelible ~~scenarios~~. When I ~~retrospect~~ them, various kinds of emotions, which defy description, often strike a chord with me.

scenario n. 可能发生的事情，设想；剧情梗概

retrospect n. 回顾，回想（in retrospect 回想起来，事后看来）回顾，回想 recall/ look back on 难以用文字描述的：indescribable; defy/beyond description 拨动心弦：strike a chord with sb. pull/tug at one’s heartstrings

> In the depths of my memory lie / exist some moments that I can never forget. Whenever I dig them up, emotions beyond description often pull at my heartstrings.

陈文伯译文：Deep in my heart have been enshrines some unforgettable scenes, any of which,

> when emerging to view, never fails to tug at my heartstrings and make them vibrate with feelings that defy description.

2. <u>日复一日</u>地上班下班，<u>平淡</u>的日子便<u>如流水一般在指缝中溜走了</u>。<u>早出晚归的行色匆匆</u>也令我无暇<u>胡思乱想</u>。

“平淡的日子”都得会，比如 simple / day-to-day / uneventful life。当然，还有其它形容词可以考虑，比如 mundane 或者 Prosaic。

> I go to work and return home every day, with time passing by like the running water sneaking from my finger seam. Due to such busy life, I cannot find time to think other things.

> As I commune to and from work day after day, the simple life just slips through my fingers like water. The hectic schedule from morning to night leaves little time to let my mind wander freely.

陈文伯译文：Humdrum days flow like water through my fingers as I go on daily trips to and from

> work - early departure and late return in such a hurry that little time is left for me to indulge in fanciful thought.

> One early morning, I was riding a bike on the wide Third Ring Road. I raised my head and happened to see an old man standing imposingly on the overpass. Quietly leaning against the guardrail, he gazed into the distance attentively. Then, the rising sun cast a red glow on him, turning his silvery hair into golden hair. He resembled a beautiful statue. From that day on, I begun to look out for him. In fact, I saw him standing there like a statue almost every morning. I had no idea who he was and what he was thinking. Judging from when and where he appeared and the solemn expressions on his face, I guessed that probably he was looking back on the ups and downs of his past; or, he was looking down at the hustle and bustle of people below for a relaxation of the mind; or, he was looking into his golden years when he would continue to shine brightly…

陈文伯译文：One early morning, however, when I was cycling along the broad Ring Road 3, I

> happened to look up at the overpass and caught a glimpse of an old man in silhouette – he stood quietly by the railings gazing far ahead. A fine statue he was, bathing in the red rising sun which gilded his hair on the edge. Since then, I had always been mindful every morning I passed there, and would always see a statue of an old man standing above. There was no telling who he was, and what there was in his mind. I could only guess from his thoughtful appearance in such a time and place that he might be going over the fading vicissitudes of his life, or enjoying a broad vista opening up in his mind as he looked down at the inexhaustible flow of traffic below, or contemplating the prospects of his golden old age which was still radiating luster.

4.<u>生命如流</u>。即使是青春，也难免吟唱些“流逝的风、流逝的梦、流逝的年年岁岁。”然而，流逝并不仅仅使人<u>黯然神伤</u>。流逝之美，正如老人与朝阳，从<u>哲学的深层感悟</u>中提炼出来，令人回味无穷。

> Life is like the running water. Even in one’s youth, he or she may sing such things like “the fleeting wind, the fleeting dream and the fleeting years.” However, the fleeting time doesn’t merely bring disappointment to people. Its beauty, just like the elderly man and the rising sun, is refined from the deep philosophical reflection, and

> Life is like flowing water. Even in their youth, people may sing songs that mourn the days, dreams and moments that have slipped away. However, the passage of time doesn’t simply leave us with sorrow. Its beauty, just like the old man bathed in the rising sun, derives from philosophical observations and offers many memorable moments for us to relish.

陈文伯译文：Life goes like water flowing. Even young people cannot help singing, “the passing

> wind, the vanishing dreams, the elapsing days and years”. But what’s going and gone, though often accompanied by sadness, has a beauty of its own. Like the old man in the rising sun, its beauty has a philosophical depth awaiting exploration by resourceful minds.

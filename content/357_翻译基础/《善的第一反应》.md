# 《善的第一反应》

## 前导引言与考情

星期三，可怕的文学翻译在敲门。

最近收到一些同学的留言，说自己练习翻译有三四个月的时间，但似乎没有看到明显的效果或者译文质量的提升。这实属正常现象。

汉英翻译学得越久，你就会发现自己知识上的盲点和漏洞到处都是。一开始以为翻译只是需要有良好的英文表达能力，包括词汇量，语法知识等等。但实际上做的材料越多，就会感觉到，翻译的知识和能力要求其实是没有边界的。

你必须有良好的中文阅读理解能力——面对一些材料，特别是一些抽象的措辞，理解的思路和最终的结果，都容易与原文希望传递的信息相差甚远。特别是原文里面有一些隐藏的含义以及语义关联，需要大家学会分析字里行间的言外之意，而不是肤浅的字面含义。这是挖掘内在含义的重要过程。

而且，还必须要拥有丰富的背景知识。很多同学做政经类的材料做久了，积累了不少相关的概念或者表达；但如果换成科技类、文化类、甚至是文学类，就感觉无从下手。俗话说得好，隔行如隔山。很多专业的概念，包括一些常见的套路表达，都需要慢慢积累。

当然，不管是什么类型的文本，什么难度的文本，语言层面的基本要求始终是不变的。**那就是：语法错误越少越好。能够做到这一点，就已经超越了70%的同学。**

### 考核指标
- **难度**：★★★★★
- **字数**：423 字
- **建议翻译时间**：50 - 60 分钟
- **翻译难点**：抽象措辞的理解、叙事断句、叙事与哲理衔接、虚拟语气处理

## 完整原文

> 当旅游大巴进入山区一个拐弯处时，车上一对情侣被窗外的美景所吸引，便招呼司机停车。他们下车后，巴士继续前行。就在这对情侣驻足欣赏山景时，只听前方轰隆一声巨响，那辆大巴被山体滑坡滚下来的巨石砸中，落入了万丈深谷。事后得知，车上乘客无一生还。惊魂甫定之后，这对情侣不无感慨地说了一句话。
> 
> 当时我们正坐在前往一座名山的旅游车上，导游小姐讲到这里，神秘地卖了一个关子，让大家猜猜看，那对情侣会怎样表达自己劫后余生的心情。
> 
> 大家给出的答案五花八门。
> 
> 在许多有关交通事故的报道中，我们经常会读到因临时变故错过班车而幸免于难的新闻。按常理，一般人都会说，幸好我们不在车上！
> 
> 不料，这个故事的答案却是：假如我们不中途下车，那辆大巴就能赶在山石滑落之前驶过，车上的人也许就不会遭遇这场劫难。
> 
> 传播这个段子的人，都把这件事看做是对人生智慧的测试，而我则认为这是对人的心灵的拷问，并为这对情侣面对灾难首先想到他人的心态由衷地敬佩。慈悲之心，虽然人皆有之，但善恶之分，往往系于一念。

## 句式精析

### PART ONE

#### 1. 当旅游大巴进入山区一个拐弯处时，车上一对情侣被窗外的美景所吸引，便招呼司机停车。
> **【参考译文】** When a tour bus rounded a bend on a mountain road, a couple onboard, fascinated by the scenery outside, asked the driver to pull over and then got off.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** The tourist bus winded its way into a mountainous area. When it was about to make a turn, two passengers—a couple of young lovers—were so attracted by the beautiful scenery that they called for a halt to get off.

- **解析**：
  - “旅游大巴”可用 `tour bus` 或 `tourist bus`。
  - “拐弯”在道路场景中，动词可选用地道的 **`round a bend`**（转过弯道）或 `make a turn`；陈文伯先生则先用 `winded its way into a mountainous area`（在山中盘旋而入），极具画面感。
  - “被窗外的美景所吸引”处理成非谓语分词定语/原因状语：`fascinated by the scenery outside` 或结果状语 `so attracted by the beautiful scenery that...`。
  - “招呼”不能直译为 `greet`（打招呼问候），真实动作是要求司机停靠：`asked the driver to pull over` 或 `called for a halt`。

---

#### 2. 他们下车后，巴士继续前行。
> **【参考译文】** The bus continued along the road.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** They were left behind and the bus went on.

- **解析**：
  - “他们下车后”可顺承并入前句或单独处理。陈文伯先生巧妙处理成被动视角 `They were left behind and the bus went on`，展现巴士把他们留下开走的情景。
  - 继续前行可用 `continued along the road` 或 `went on`。

---

#### 3. 就在这对情侣驻足欣赏山景时，只听前方轰隆一声巨响，那辆大巴被山体滑坡滚下来的巨石砸中，落入了万丈深谷。
> **【参考译文】** The couple stood there and admired the view, but suddenly they heard a rumbling sound. Before their eyes, a huge boulder rolled down the hillside, struck the bus and pushed it into a deep valley.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** As they were admiring the landscape, they heard a loud boom ahead the road. It was a huge rock rolling down in a landslide hit the bus and fell with it into a deep valley.

- **解析**：
  - “驻足欣赏山景”：`stood there and admired the view` 或从句 `As they were admiring the landscape`。
  - “轰隆一声巨响”：象声词与听觉描写，可用 `a rumbling sound`（轰隆声）或 `a loud boom ahead`。
  - “山体滑坡滚下来的巨石砸中”：理清施动逻辑。山体滑坡 `landslide`，巨石 `boulder / huge rock`。动作链分解为：滚下山坡（`rolled down the hillside / in a landslide`）→ 砸中大巴（`struck / hit the bus`）→ 坠入深谷（`pushed it into a deep valley / fell with it into a deep valley`）。
  - “万丈深谷”无需直译万丈，白话即 `a deep valley` / `a precipitous chasm`。

---

#### 4. 事后得知，车上乘客无一生还。
> **【参考译文】** The couple learned afterwards that all of the passengers died.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** The lovers were greatly struck by the news that no passengers on board survived.

- **解析**：
  - “事后得知”：中文无主句，逻辑主语是这对情侣，补全为 `The couple learned afterwards that...`；陈译以消息对他们的心理冲击为核心：`The lovers were greatly struck by the news that...`。
  - “无一生还”：`all of the passengers died` 或更精炼地道的 `no passengers on board survived`。

---

#### 5. 惊魂甫定之后，这对情侣不无感慨地说了一句话。
> **【参考译文】** Reeling from the shock, the couple said something emotionally.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** When they calmed down, they sighed and expressed their feelings in a few words.

- **解析**：
  - “惊魂甫定”：意即从惊恐与震撼中缓过神来。地道成语表达可用分词 `Reeling from the shock`（从震惊中回过神），或意译 `When they calmed down`。
  - “不无感慨地”：形容情绪饱满地感慨，可用 `emotionally / feelingly`，或动词化拆解为 `they sighed and expressed their feelings in a few words`。

---

### PART TWO

#### 当时我们正坐在前往一座名山的旅游车上，导游小姐讲到这里，神秘地卖了一个关子，让大家猜猜看，那对情侣会怎样表达自己劫后余生的心情。
> **【参考译文】** The story was shared by a guide with us while we were sitting on a bus bound for a renowned mountain. Instead of directly telling the answer, she asked us to guess what the couple would say after escaping a close brush with death.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** The above story was told by a woman guide when we were riding a tourist bus to a scenic mountain. She left us in a mysterious suspense and asked us to guess what the lovers said immediately after the catastrophe that they had narrowly escaped.

- **解析**：
  - **段落衔接与信息流**：上一段刚讲完情侣的故事，本段如果直接按中文“当时我们正坐在...”直译，英文读者会困惑叙事视角的突然跳跃。因此参考译文与陈译均先以“故事”承上启下：`The story was shared by a guide with us...` / `The above story was told by a woman guide...`。
  - “前往一座名山的旅游车”：`a bus bound for a renowned mountain` 或 `riding a tourist bus to a scenic mountain`。
  - “神秘地卖了一个关子”：地道表达是留出悬念 `left us in a suspense / kept us in suspense`，或解释动作 `Instead of directly telling the answer`。
  - “劫后余生”：不能简单写成 `survive the accident`，因为两人并没有经历大巴坠谷。准确理解是“死里逃生 / 逃过一劫”：`escaping a close brush with death`（从死神擦肩而过中逃脱）或 `the catastrophe that they had narrowly escaped`（死里逃生的灾难）。

---

### PART THREE

#### 大家给出的答案五花八门。
> **【参考译文】** The passengers gave a bunch of different answers.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** On our bus passengers' response to the guide were various; all possible guesses were given.

- **解析**：
  - “五花八门”：指答案繁多各异。口语/叙事化可用 `a bunch of different answers`；书面表达可用 `were various / varied widely`。陈文伯先生在此补全了主客体：`passengers' response to the guide were various; all possible guesses were given`。

---

### PART FOUR

#### 1. 在许多有关交通事故的报道中，我们经常会读到因临时变故错过班车而幸免于难的新闻。
> **【参考译文】** In news reports about bus accidents, we often read stories in which some lucky passengers missed the bus for personal reasons and thus avoided the accidents.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** Often we read reports about traffic accidents, among which there were lucky ones who missed the bus and avoided the disaster.

- **解析**：
  - “交通事故的报道”：结合语境可特指客车/大巴事故 `news reports about bus accidents`，亦可泛指 `reports about traffic accidents`。
  - “幸免于难”：引出侥幸者 `lucky passengers / lucky ones who missed the bus and avoided the disaster`。
  - “临时变故”：化虚为实，通常指个人突发事务耽搁，故译为 `for personal reasons` 或直接体现在 `missed the bus` 的动作后果中。

---

#### 2. 按常理，一般人都会说，幸好我们不在车上！
> **【参考译文】** In such cases, they would typically say, “Fortunately, I was not in the bus.”  
> **【陈文伯译文】** Those people usually uttered a sigh of relief. "Fortunately I was not on board."

- **解析**：
  - “按常理”：说人话即通常情况下 `typically / usually / as a rule`。
  - “幸好我们不在车上”：这是具体说话人设身处地的引语，中文习惯用“我们”，但具体个人说话时通常用单数 `"Fortunately, I was not on board / in the bus."`。陈文伯先生加了一个生动的伴随动作 `uttered a sigh of relief`（舒了一口气），极其传神。

---

### PART FIVE

#### 不料，这个故事的答案却是：假如我们不中途下车，那辆大巴就能赶在山石滑落之前驶过，车上的人也许就不会遭遇这场劫难。
> **【参考译文】** The guide told us a surprising answer - the couple said, “If we hadn’t gotten off the bus, it might have passed the spot before the boulder came tumbling down, and other passengers might have escaped the disaster.”  
> **【陈文伯译文】** But the answer was quite out of our expectation. "If we hadn't got down and delayed the bus, it would have passed the dangerous spot and all the passengers would have been saved."

- **解析**：
  - “不料”：`To our surprise / But the answer was quite out of our expectation`。
  - “故事的答案”：中文拟人化表达，实际指“情侣给出的真实回答”，翻译时需明确说话人。
  - **核心语法考点 · 虚拟语气（与过去事实相反）**：
    - 条件句：`If we had not gotten off the bus (and delayed it)...`（过去完成时）；
    - 主句：`it would / might have passed the spot... and all the passengers would have been saved / might have escaped the disaster.`（情态动词 + have done）。
    - 这里的虚拟语气是衡量译者英文功底的硬指标，绝对不能误写为真实条件句。

---

### PART SIX

#### 1. 传播这个段子的人，都把这件事看做是对人生智慧的测试，而我则认为这是对人的心灵的拷问，并为这对情侣面对灾难首先想到他人的心态由衷地敬佩。
> **【参考译文】** People who share this story probably see others' answers as a test of their wisdom of life. In my eyes, however, it is a test of our souls, and I honestly respect the couple who put the interests of others above their own.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** The story passed from person to person as a sort of wit test, but I think otherwise. It actually serves as an examination of a person's mind. I pay my heartfelt respect to the couple of lovers who, instead of feeling lucky for their narrow escape, blamed themselves for causing the loss of lives inadvertently.

- **解析**：
  - “段子”：并非逗乐的笑话，而是指这个耐人寻味的故事 `story`；“传播”可用 `share / pass from person to person`。
  - “人生智慧的测试”：`a test of wisdom of life` 或 `wit test`。
  - “心灵的拷问”：“拷问”在此是比喻义，不能用动词 interrogate，名词化处理为 `a test of our souls` 或 `an examination of a person's mind`。
  - “首先想到他人”：挖掘深层语义，即在危难关头先考虑别人的生命安危，置他人利益于个人之上：`put the interests of others above their own`；陈文伯先生则正反对比剖析：`instead of feeling lucky for their narrow escape, blamed themselves for causing the loss of lives inadvertently`，文采斐然。
  - “由衷地敬佩”：`honestly respect` 或 `pay my heartfelt respect to`。

---

#### 2. 慈悲之心，虽然人皆有之，但善恶之分，往往系于一念。
> **【参考译文】** Compassion is a widely shared trait, but the goodness or evil in a person's soul is often determined by a flashing thought.  
> **【陈文伯译文】** People all have a heart for mercy, but it is often a flashing thought across one's mind that decides whether it is good or evil.

- **解析**：
  - “慈悲之心”：`compassion / a heart for mercy / empathy`。
  - “善恶之分”：`the goodness or evil` 或 `whether it is good or evil`。
  - “系于一念”：抽象哲思语言化简，意指善与恶的分野往往取决于脑海中一瞬间闪过的那道念头。地道处理：`determined by a flashing thought` 或强调句 `it is often a flashing thought across one's mind that decides...`。

## 原文与参考译文汇总

### 完整中文原文

当旅游大巴进入山区一个拐弯处时，车上一对情侣被窗外的美景所吸引，便招呼司机停车。他们下车后，巴士继续前行。就在这对情侣驻足欣赏山景时，只听前方轰隆一声巨响，那辆大巴被山体滑坡滚下来的巨石砸中，落入了万丈深谷。事后得知，车上乘客无一生还。惊魂甫定之后，这对情侣不无感慨地说了一句话。

当时我们正坐在前往一座名山的旅游车上，导游小姐讲到这里，神秘地卖了一个关子，让大家猜猜看，那对情侣会怎样表达自己劫后余生的心情。

大家给出的答案五花八门。

在许多有关交通事故的报道中，我们经常会读到因临时变故错过班车而幸免于难的新闻。按常理，一般人都会说，幸好我们不在车上！

不料，这个故事的答案却是：假如我们不中途下车，那辆大巴就能赶在山石滑落之前驶过，车上的人也许就不会遭遇这场劫难。

传播这个段子的人，都把这件事看做是对人生智慧的测试，而我则认为这是对人的心灵的拷问，并为这对情侣面对灾难首先想到他人的心态由衷地敬佩。慈悲之心，虽然人皆有之，但善恶之分，往往系于一念。

### XXLIN 参考译文

When a tour bus rounded a bend on a mountain road, a couple onboard, fascinated by the scenery outside, asked the driver to pull over and then got off. The bus continued along the road. The couple stood there and admired the view, but suddenly they heard a rumbling sound. Before their eyes, a huge boulder rolled down the hillside, struck the bus and pushed it into a deep valley. The couple learned afterwards that all of the passengers died. Reeling from the shock, the couple said something emotionally.

The story was shared by a guide with us while we were sitting on a bus bound for a renowned mountain. Instead of directly telling the answer, she asked us to guess what the couple would say after escaping a close brush with death.

The passengers gave a bunch of different answers.

In news reports about bus accidents, we often read stories in which some lucky passengers missed the bus for personal reasons and thus avoided the accidents. In such cases, they would typically say, “Fortunately, I was not in the bus.”

The guide told us a surprising answer - the couple said, “If we hadn’t gotten off the bus, it might have passed the spot before the boulder came tumbling down, and other passengers might have escaped the disaster.”

People who share this story probably see others' answers as a test of their wisdom of life. In my eyes, however, it is a test of our souls, and I honestly respect the couple who put the interests of others above their own. Compassion is a widely shared trait, but the goodness or evil in a person's soul is often determined by a flashing thought.

### 陈文伯经典译文

The tourist bus winded its way into a mountainous area. When it was about to make a turn, two passengers—a couple of young lovers—were so attracted by the beautiful scenery that they called for a halt to get off. They were left behind and the bus went on. As they were admiring the landscape, they heard a loud boom ahead the road. It was a huge rock rolling down in a landslide hit the bus and fell with it into a deep valley. The lovers were greatly struck by the news that no passengers on board survived. When they calmed down, they sighed and expressed their feelings in a few words.

The above story was told by a woman guide when we were riding a tourist bus to a scenic mountain. She left us in a mysterious suspense and asked us to guess what the lovers said immediately after the catastrophe that they had narrowly escaped.

On our bus passengers' response to the guide were various; all possible guesses were given.

Often we read reports about traffic accidents, among which there were lucky ones who missed the bus and avoided the disaster. Those people usually uttered a sigh of relief. "Fortunately I was not on board."

But the answer was quite out of our expectation. "If we hadn't got down and delayed the bus, it would have passed the dangerous spot and all the passengers would have been saved."

The story passed from person to person as a sort of wit test, but I think otherwise. It actually serves as an examination of a person's mind. I pay my heartfelt respect to the couple of lovers who, instead of feeling lucky for their narrow escape, blamed themselves for causing the loss of lives inadvertently. People all have a heart for mercy, but it is often a flashing thought across one's mind that decides whether it is good or evil.

## 重点表达积累

1. **拐弯（车辆）**：`round a bend` / `make a turn`
2. **停车靠边**：`pull over` / `call for a halt`
3. **盘旋而入**：`wind one's way into...`
4. **轰隆一声巨响**：`a rumbling sound` / `a loud boom`
5. **巨石**：`a huge boulder` / `a huge rock`
6. **万丈深谷**：`a deep valley` / `a precipitous chasm`
7. **无一生还**：`no passengers on board survived`
8. **惊魂甫定**：`reeling from the shock` / `when they calmed down`
9. **开往...的巴士**：`a bus bound for...` / `riding a bus to...`
10. **卖关子 / 留悬念**：`keep sb in suspense` / `leave sb in suspense`
11. **劫后余生 / 死里逃生**：`escape a close brush with death` / `narrowly escape a catastrophe`
12. **五花八门**：`a bunch of different answers` / `be various`
13. **松了一口气**：`utter a sigh of relief`
14. **与过去相反的虚拟语气**：`If sb had done..., sb would / might have done...`
15. **出乎意料**：`out of one's expectation` / `to one's surprise`
16. **人生智慧的测试**：`a test of wisdom of life` / `a sort of wit test`
17. **心灵的拷问**：`a test of our souls` / `an examination of a person's mind`
18. **由衷的敬佩**：`pay one's heartfelt respect to sb`
19. **慈悲之心**：`compassion` / `a heart for mercy`
20. **系于一念**：`be determined by a flashing thought across one's mind`

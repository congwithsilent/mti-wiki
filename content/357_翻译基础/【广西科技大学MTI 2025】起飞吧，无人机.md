## 完整原文

> 在今年早些时候的《政府工作报告》中，中国总理李强提到了培育新的经济增长引擎。值得特别一提的是，他提到了“低空经济”，这一表达让人联想到了“飞行汽车”，这对某些人来说可能看起来像是科幻小说里的情景。但在这一领域，中国的发展速度比大多数国家都要快。飞行汽车或电动垂直起降 (eVTOL)车辆的制造商己经获得了政府的批准。
> 
> 这些车辆通常看起来像非常大的消费级无人机。有些设计为自动驾驶，不需要驾驶员操控。其中，由广东省亿航公司生产的 EH216-S 机型于 4 月获得中国民用航空局（CAAC) 颁发的“生产证书”，意味着该公司可以开始进行大规模生产。EH216-S 是世界上第一款获得此类监管批准的飞行汽车。
> 
> 亿航在中国有竞争对手。总部位于上海的 AutoFlight 公司的电动垂直起降飞行器于 3月获得了中国民用航空局的“型号合格证”，标志着其产品设计获得批准。同样位于广东的小鹏汽车所生产的一款车型也正在进行型号认证。另一热门竞争者是御风未来 Vertaxi, 该品牌的电动垂直起降飞行器于 10 月进行了首飞。
> 
> 中国在这个领域有两大优势。首先是该国在电动汽车技术方面的优势，其中一些是可以从汽车迁移应用到飞行汽车上的。此外，电池也尤为重要。电动垂直起降飞行器使用的电池必须轻巧且容量够大。而中国则是世界上最大的电池制造商。其中，领头羊宁德时代（CATL）正在努力应对飞行汽车带来的挑战。第二个优势是政府的积极支持。中国的目标是到 2025 年在珠江三角洲和长江三角洲等地区建立“低空经济示范区”。中国政府己经在考虑，一旦无人机和电动垂直起降飞行器真正起飞，该如何管理空域。

## 句式精析

### PART ONE

#### 1. 在今年早些时候的《政府工作报告》中，中国总理李强提到了培育新的经济增长引擎。
> **【参考译文】** In the Government Work Report published earlier this year, Chinese Premier Li Qiang called for efforts to foster new engines of economic growth.

- **解析**：
  - “今年早些时候”是明显的英文表达 `earlier this year`。它与“《政府工作报告》”的关系需要补出非谓语动词：`published / released earlier this year`。
  - 英文中严禁在书名上使用中文书名号《 》，首字母大写即可：`the Government Work Report`。
  - 中国总理的标准官方英译必须用 **`Premier`**（不能用西方内阁制的 prime minister）。
  - “培育新的经济增长引擎”属于政经高频套话：`foster new engines of economic growth`（“培育”用 `foster / cultivate`）。

---

#### 2. 值得特别一提的是，他提到了“低空经济”，这一表达让人联想到了“飞行汽车”，这对某些人来说可能看起来像是科幻小说里的情景。
> **【参考译文】** Notably, he highlighted “the low-altitude economy”, a term reminiscent of flying cars that seem to exist only in science fiction.

- **解析**：
  - “值得特别一提的是”：简练副词 `Notably`，或从句 `what deserves special attention is that`。
  - “低空经济”：时政热词 **`the low-altitude economy`**。
  - “这一表达让人联想到……”：使用名词同位语 + 形容词短语 `a term reminiscent of...`（比单纯用 which reminds people 更具地道书面语色彩）。
  - “科幻小说里的情景”：`that seem to exist only in science fiction`（通过定语从句生动活现“情景”之意，无需机械死译 scene）。

---

#### 3. 但在这一领域，中国的发展速度比大多数国家都要快。
> **【参考译文】** In this sector, however, China has outpaced many other countries.

- **解析**：
  - “发展速度比……快”：如果直译为 `be faster than` 较为平庸，高级动词优先推荐 **`outpace`**（超越、跑赢）；相关动词还有 `outperform`（表现优于）。

---

#### 4. 飞行汽车或电动垂直起降 (eVTOL)车辆的制造商己经获得了政府的批准。
> **【参考译文】** In fact, some Chinese manufacturers of flying cars, or electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, have already obtained government approval for production.

- **解析**：
  - “电动垂直起降飞行器”：首次出现专业术语需写出全称与缩写：**`electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft`**。
  - “获得了政府的批准”：`obtained government approval for production`。

---

### PART TWO

#### 1. 这些车辆通常看起来像非常大的消费级无人机。
> **【参考译文】** These cars resemble massive consumer drones.

- **解析**：
  - “看起来像”：动词 `resemble`。
  - “消费级”：名词作定语修饰 **`consumer drones`**（军用/工业级对应 commercial / industrial / military drones；专业简称 UAV = Unmanned Aerial Vehicle）。

---

#### 2. 有些设计为自动驾驶，不需要驾驶员操控。
> **【参考译文】** Some are designed to operate autonomously, without the need for human pilots.

- **解析**：
  - “自动驾驶”：副词修饰动词 **`operate autonomously`** 或形容词 `autonomous`。
  - “不需要驾驶员操控”：逻辑上作为伴随状语处理为介词短语 **`without the need for human pilots`**，结构极其紧凑。

---

#### 3. 其中，由广东省亿航公司生产的 EH216-S 机型于 4 月获得中国民用航空局（CAAC) 颁发的“生产证书”，意味着该公司可以开始进行大规模生产。
> **【参考译文】** For example, the EH216-S model manufactured by Guangdong-based EHang obtained a production license from the Civil Aviation Administration of China in April, allowing it to begin large-scale production.

- **解析**：
  - 企业专名：亿航的官方英文名称为 **`EHang`**；“广东的”作定语可写为 `Guangdong-based`。
  - “中国民用航空局”：官方标准专名 **`Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC)`**。
  - “生产证书”：行业官方文件为 **`production license`**。
  - 后半句“意味着……”：处理为现在分词作结果状语 `allowing it to begin large-scale production`。

---

#### 4. EH216-S 是世界上第一款获得此类监管批准的飞行汽车。
> **【参考译文】** In fact, the EH216-S is the world’s first flying vehicle that received regulatory approval.

- **解析**：
  - “监管批准”：专业政经固定搭配 **`regulatory approval`**。

---

### PART THREE

#### 1. 亿航在中国有竞争对手。
> **【参考译文】** EHang is not without competition in China.

- **解析**：
  - 巧妙采用双重否定 **`be not without competition`**，比生硬直译 `has competitors` 更加地道老练。

---

#### 2. 总部位于上海的 AutoFlight 公司的电动垂直起降飞行器于 3月获得了中国民用航空局的“型号合格证”，标志着其产品设计获得批准。
> **【参考译文】** AutoFlight, headquartered in Shanghai, gained a type certificate for its eVTOL aircraft this March, meaning that its product design has received official approval.

- **解析**：
  - “型号合格证”：航空制造业固定适航审定术语 **`type certificate`**。
  - “标志着……”：分词结果状语 `meaning that...`。

---

#### 3. 同样位于广东的小鹏汽车所生产的一款车型也正在进行型号认证。
> **【参考译文】** In addition, Xiaopeng Motors in Guangdong is seeking type approval for its model of flying cars.

- **解析**：
  - 小鹏汽车：**`Xiaopeng Motors`** 或 `XPeng`。
  - “进行型号认证”：转换为动作目标 **`seeking type approval`**。

---

#### 4. 另一热门竞争者是御风未来 Vertaxi, 该品牌的电动垂直起降飞行器于 10 月进行了首飞。
> **【参考译文】** Another strong competitor is Vertaxi whose flying vehicle made its maiden flight in October.

- **解析**：
  - “热门竞争者”：不宜硬译为 hot competitor，地道英文为 **`strong competitor / formidable rival`**。
  - “首飞”：航空专业术语为 **`maiden flight`**（切忌造词 first fly）。
  - 后半句通过 `whose` 定语从句一气呵成。

---

### PART FOUR

#### 1. 中国在这个领域有两大优势。首先是该国在电动汽车技术方面的优势，其中一些是可以从汽车迁移应用到飞行汽车上的。
> **【参考译文】** China stands out for two notable advantages in this sector. First, some of its leading edge in electric vehicles can be transferred and applied to flying vehicles.

- **解析**：
  - “有优势”：`stands out for two notable advantages` 或 `boasts two key advantages`。
  - “技术优势”：替换为高阶词 **`leading edge / competitive edge`**。
  - “迁移应用”：动词并列 **`be transferred and applied to`**。

---

#### 2. 此外，电池也尤为重要。电动垂直起降飞行器使用的电池必须轻巧且容量够大。而中国则是世界上最大的电池制造商。
> **【参考译文】** For example, batteries used in eVTOL aircraft need to be lightweight and offer sufficient capacity, while China is the world’s large battery producer.

- **解析**：
  - 原文第三句“此外，电池也尤为重要”属于铺垫泛语，译文中与后句具体需求合并：举例说明电池需 `lightweight and offer sufficient capacity`。
  - “容量够大”：处理为动宾搭配 **`offer sufficient capacity`**，比单纯形容词更显工业精确度。
  - 通过 `while` 对比引出中国作为电池大国的行业地位。

---

#### 3. 其中，领头羊宁德时代（CATL）正在努力应对飞行汽车带来的挑战。
> **【参考译文】** In particular, the leading manufacturer Contemporary Amperex Technology (CATL) is working to address challenges brought by flying vehicles.

- **解析**：
  - “领头羊”：**`the leading manufacturer / market leader / pacesetter`**。
  - 宁德时代全称：**`Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited (CATL)`**。
  - “应对挑战”：**`address challenges`**。

---

#### 4. 第二个优势是政府的积极支持。中国的目标是到 2025 年在珠江三角洲和长江三角洲等地区建立“低空经济示范区”。中国政府己经在考虑，一旦无人机和电动垂直起降飞行器真正起飞，该如何管理空域。
> **【参考译文】** Second, the government provides strong support for the industry. China aims to build demonstration areas for the low-altitude economy in the Pearl River Delta and the Yangtze River Delta by 2025, and the government has begun to consider approaches to airspace management as the drone and eVTOL sectors take off.

- **解析**：
  - 地理专名：珠江三角洲 **`the Pearl River Delta`**；长江三角洲 **`the Yangtze River Delta`**。
  - “示范区”：**`demonstration areas`**。
  - “空域管理”：**`airspace management`**。
  - “起飞”：双关语 **`take off`**（既指飞行器升空，又指产业蓬勃兴起）。

### 完整参考译文
> In the Government Work Report published earlier this year, Chinese Premier Li Qiang called for efforts to foster new engines of economic growth. Notably, he highlighted “the low-altitude economy”, a term reminiscent of flying cars that seem to exist only in science fiction. In this sector, however, China has outpaced many other countries. In fact, some Chinese manufacturers of flying cars, or electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, have already obtained government approval for production.  
> These cars resemble massive consumer drones. Some are designed to operate autonomously, without the need for human pilots. For example, the EH216-S model manufactured by Guangdong-based EHang obtained a production license from the Civil Aviation Administration of China in April, allowing it to begin large-scale production. In fact, the EH216-S is the world’s first flying vehicle that received regulatory approval.  
> EHang is not without competition in China. AutoFlight, headquartered in Shanghai, gained a type certificate for its eVTOL aircraft this March, meaning that its product design has received official approval. In addition, Xiaopeng Motors in Guangdong is seeking type approval for its model of flying cars. Another strong competitor is Vertaxi whose flying vehicle made its maiden flight in October.  
> China stands out for two notable advantages in this sector. First, some of its leading edge in electric vehicles can be transferred and applied to flying vehicles. For example, batteries used in eVTOL aircraft need to be lightweight and offer sufficient capacity, while China is the world’s large battery producer. In particular, the leading manufacturer Contemporary Amperex Technology (CATL) is working to address challenges brought by flying vehicles. Second, the government provides strong support for the industry. China aims to build demonstration areas for the low-altitude economy in the Pearl River Delta and the Yangtze River Delta by 2025, and the government has begun to consider approaches to airspace management as the drone and eVTOL sectors take off.

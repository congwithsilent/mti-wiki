# 《谈抄袭》

星期三到了，文学翻译随之而来。

今天的材料内容不算少，难度也不算低。所以今天的午饭又会显得格外地咸。

对于文学翻译，相信不少同学会感到特别的害怕，甚至是抵触。可能有一些同学试图背诵张培基的散文译文，想着如果在课上遇到原文，可以直接把张先生的译文写上去。

实践证明这种想法是不恰当的，也是不健康的，投机取巧的心理太重了。

应该前年还是大前年的时候，天津某院校的汉英题目就出自于张培基的散文选，就有不少同学把张培基的译文写了上去。遗憾的是，都没有得高分。

原因非常简单：翻译，要看译者的主观能动性，不是考察背诵能力。一股脑地复制别人的译文，没有个人思考的过程，本身也是一种抄袭。

文学翻译比较难，除了中文原文本身的理解难度比较高以外，对于大家的另外一项隐性要求，是文学素养。

如果平时连文学作品都不怎么看，那么让你去做文学汉英翻译，难度堪比登天。毕竟，你连猴子都没有见过，怎么去画猴子？

而且，文学翻译的学习效果，也需要很长的时间才能够显现出来。相比之下，政经类材料因为套话多，积累多了，慢慢也就会了，而文学翻译材料本身表达多样，需要积累的表达更多，需要大量的时间和精力。

总之，慢慢学。

- **难度**：★★★★★
- **字数**：417 字
- **建议翻译时间**：50 - 60 分钟
- **翻译难点**：抽象措辞的理解

## 完整原文

> 若是“抄袭”之作，无论你怎样改头换面，都会被看出来。因为原作那个“魂儿”，你变不了。你以为你把它变成你的作品灵魂了，就是原创了。可惜你不是。
> 比如某些画作，一看，就知道它的哪一笔是从哪来的，哪个人物的哪个表情是从哪来的。
> 有幅画，画了一位农民工，蹲在都市一隅，望着某一奢侈场所进进出出的人。那些人，或大肚便便，或婀娜妖冶，或春风满面，或踌躇满志，一个个都很生动。那个农民工画得也很逼真。表情，眼神，衣着，姿势等等都很到位，真实地反映了“农民工”的生存状态。那些建筑，都是他们一砖一瓦砌出来的，但装修完之后，他就再也没有机会进去了，更别提进去“享受”了。
> 就画作本身而言，立意和画技的确不错，但若授予某一级别的“创作奖”，似乎就不够格了，因为这幅画是“抄袭”之作。
> 外国曾经有位画家，画过一个马戏团的小丑，疲惫地蹲在剧场的一个角落里，望着散场离去的人。那些离去的人，没有一个在意这个刚刚还给他们带来过笑声的小丑。
> 这就是“抄袭”，不是“借鉴”。
## 我的译文

A plagrized work is easy to figure out, no matter how the author packages it. Because you can't change the "spirit" of original work. Sometimes you may think that you have transformed the spirit into your own soul and produce original works. However, you can't.
Like some paintings, you can easily figure out the source of touches, characters and their facial expressions as you put the sight on.
Take a painting as example. It describes a rural worker squatting at there and watching the crowds in a luxury place. These crowds, some of them are fat, or alluring, or confident, or ambitious, are painted lively; The rural worker is also painted vividly, with the facial expression, eyesight, outfits and posture are all described well, reflecting the living situation of rural workers. These buildings were built by their hands, however, they didn't have the chance to enter that place after decoration, let alone to enjoy things inside.
The skill and idea can be realized as excellent if we only focus on the painting. However, if we want to honor it with some awards, the painting is incapable, for it is a plagrized work. Why?
Before that, a foreign painter drew a painting about a clown in a circus, who squatting at a corner of theatre with tiredness and watching the leaving audience. None of them would pay attention to a clown, who even just brought happiness and laughter to them.
See? That's plagrization, not reference.

## 句式精析

### PART ONE

#### 1. 若是“抄袭”之作，无论你怎样改头换面，都会被看出来。

> **【参考译文】** A plagiarized work, no matter how fancifully it is packaged, will be detected.

“抄袭”的英文是什么？大家从小到大，敢抠着脚丫子说自己从来没有抄袭过别人的东西吗？

我承认，我小时候抄过同学的作业！

“改头换面”可以理解成：包装。比如：package。至于“看出来”，可以理解成：看破，识破。动词的话，可以是 see through 或者 detect。

---

#### 2. 因为原作那个“魂儿”，你变不了。

> **【参考译文】** Why? Because the soul of the original can’t be changed.

“魂儿”译不出来，可能人就没有魂儿了。Soul。

“变不了”含义清晰：无法改变。

---

#### 3. 你以为你把它变成你的作品灵魂了，就是原创了。

> **【参考译文】** You may believe that by transforming it into yours, you can produce an original work.

“它”指代的是什么？当然是原文的灵魂。

此处，要注意前后两句话的语义关联。前面明显是一种方式，可以处理成方式状语。后半句的“就是原创”似乎没有形式主语，可以添加。也可以和前半句统一主语，都变成“你”。此时，需要考虑添加合适的谓语动词，比如 produce 或者 create。

---

#### 4. 可惜你不是。

> **【参考译文】** In fact, you can’t.

原文只是说“不是”，但并非一定是 you are not。因为此处的动词具体是什么，取决于前一句的动词选择。

---

### PART TWO

#### 比如某些画作，一看，就知道它的哪一笔是从哪来的，哪个人物的哪个表情是从哪来的。

> **【参考译文】** For example, in examining a plagiarized painting, we can easily discover where its certain brushstrokes come from and where particular facial expressions of the characters in it come from.

本句的内容较多，但要注意表达视角的跳跃性。

“某些画作”和后面的“一看”完全可以合并翻译。呆毛，此处要注意的是，“画作”是泛泛地指所有画作吗？当然不是，而是指“抄袭别人的画作”。

“笔”不是 pen，而是 drawing 或者 stroke。

本句里面的“哪个”怎么处理？直接就是 which。或者再简单点，就是省略不译。

---

### PART THREE

#### 1. 有幅画，画了一位农民工，蹲在都市一隅，望着某一奢侈场所进进出出的人。

> **【参考译文】** For example, there is a painting that depicts a rural worker. He is squatting at a corner of a city and watching people entering and leaving a luxury building.

“农民工”不会的话，以后毕业了怎么到工地上搬砖？

“蹲”可以 squat。“隅”可以是 corner 或者 a certain place。“望着”的动词非常多，比如 look at, stare 或者 watch 或者 observe，哪个更恰当呢？

“进进出出的人”完全可以直译的。

---

#### 2. 那些人，或大肚便便，或婀娜妖冶，或春风满面，或踌躇满志，一个个都很生动。

> **【参考译文】** Some of these people are big-bellied, some dressed seductively, some all smiles and some enormously proud.

“大肚便便”指的是身体特征，可以处理成 have a big belly 不？完全可以，或者用形容词 big-bellied。

“婀娜妖冶”明显修饰女性，可以处理成 alluring 或者 coquettish。实在不会，就解释出来：穿着妖艳。比如：seductively dressed。

“春风满面，或踌躇满志”同样需要解释出来。前者可以是：be all smiles；后者呢？enormously proud。

---

#### 3. 那个农民工画得也很逼真。

> **【参考译文】** The rural worker himself is also vividly portrayed.

“逼真”可以是 vividly，没啥难度。

---

#### 4. 表情，眼神，衣着，姿势等等都很到位，真实地反映了“农民工”的生存状态。

> **【参考译文】** His facial expressions, the look in his eyes, his clothes and his posture are all realistically reproduced, offering an insight into the lives of rural workers.

“表情，眼神，衣着，姿势”是谁的？原文形式上没说，但必须要加物主代词。

“眼神”简单点处理，eyes。当然，也有完整的版本：the look in his eyes。“很到位”不是 in position，需要说人话：非常符合现实。可以考虑最简单的表达：realistically。

“生存状态”简单点，life status 或者 life。

---

#### 5. 那些建筑，都是他们一砖一瓦砌出来的，但装修完之后，他就再也没有机会进去了，更别提进去“享受”了。

> **【参考译文】** These luxury buildings are built by them brick by brick; after decoration, however, they have no opportunity to enter them again, let alone have fun there.

“一砖一瓦”是具体指代，但此处似乎不是真的指砖瓦。建议处理成：亲手。如果想直译，建议用 brick by brick。

“享受”是 enjoy themselves 吗？可以，也可以考虑 have fun。

---

### PART FOUR

#### 就画作本身而言，立意和画技的确不错，但若授予某一级别的“创作奖”，似乎就不够格了，因为这幅画是“抄袭”之作。

> **【参考译文】** Both the idea and skills that the painting demonstrates are excellent, but it is not qualified to gain awards for excellence in art production. After all, it is a plagiarized work.

“就画作本身而言”看起来是个状语，但本质含义非常少：这个画作。含义上完全可以和后面的“立意和画技”合并起来翻译，即充当定语。

“立意”是啥？idea。“画技” skills。“不错”用 good，多少有些不尊重大学生的表达能力。

“创作奖”此处可能压根不知道这个奖项的英文，但完全不用害怕。解释出来：向优秀作品颁发的奖项。

后面的内容就是套话了。

---

### PART FIVE

#### 1. 外国曾经有位画家，画过一个马戏团的小丑，疲惫地蹲在剧场的一个角落里，望着散场离去的人。

> **【参考译文】** A foreign artist had created a painting in which an exhausted circus clown, sitting at a corner of the theater, watches audiences leaving after the end of the show.

“马戏团的小丑”译不出来，你自己就是那个小丑。

“疲惫地”可以处理成定语，比如 exhausted。也可以处理成状语，tiredly。

“散场离去的人”此处不建议用啥 people，因为此处的“人”是具体的人，可以考虑 audience。至于“散场离去”指的是“离开剧场”，简单不？

---

#### 2. 那些离去的人，没有一个在意这个刚刚还给他们带来过笑声的小丑。

> **【参考译文】** None of them, however, pay attention to the clown who has brought them so much laughter.

“那些离去的人”和前面的内容重复，完全可以用代词表达。

后面的内容没啥可说的，都是送分的。

---

### PART SIX

#### 这就是“抄袭”，不是“借鉴”。

> **【参考译文】** Therefore, the first painting represents a form of plagiarism, rather than a legitimate borrowing of ideas.

“抄袭”作为名词，可以考虑 plagiarism。而“借鉴”，可以考虑 draw inspiration 或者 borrow。

此处的主语“这”指的是什么？可以是画作本身，也可以是画作体现的一种行为。

### 原文

> 若是“抄袭”之作，无论你怎样改头换面，都会被看出来。因为原作那个“魂儿”，你变不了。你以为你把它变成你的作品灵魂了，就是原创了。可惜你不是。比如某些画作，一看，就知道它的哪一笔是从哪来的，哪个人物的哪个表情是从哪来的。有幅画，画了一位农民工，蹲在都市一隅，望着某一奢侈场所进进出出的人。那些人，或大肚便便，或婀娜妖冶，或春风满面，或踌躇满志，一个个都很生动。那个农民工画得也很逼真。表情，眼神，衣着，姿势等等都很到位，真实地反映了“农民工”的生存状态。那些建筑，都是他们一砖一瓦砌出来的，但装修完之后，他就再也没有机会进去了，更别提进去“享受”了。就画作本身而言，立意和画技的确不错，但若授予某一级别的“创作奖”，似乎就不够格了，因为这幅画是“抄袭”之作。外国曾经有位画家，画过一个马戏团的小丑，疲惫地蹲在剧场的一个角落里，望着散场离去的人。那些离去的人，没有一个在意这个刚刚还给他们带来过笑声的小丑。这就是“抄袭”，不是“借鉴”。

### 参考译文

> A plagiarized work, no matter how fancifully it is packaged, will be detected. Why? Because the soul of the original can’t be changed. You may believe that by transforming it into yours, you can produce an original work. In fact, you can’t. For example, in examining a plagiarized painting, we can easily discover where its certain brushstrokes come from and where particular facial expressions of the characters in it come from. For example, there is a painting that depicts a rural worker. He is squatting at a corner of a city and watching people entering and leaving a luxury building. Some of these people are big-bellied, some dressed seductively, some all smiles and some enormously proud. The rural worker himself is also vividly portrayed. His facial expressions, the look in his eyes, his clothes and his posture are all realistically reproduced, offering an insight into the lives of rural workers. These luxury buildings are built by them brick by brick; after decoration, however, they have no opportunity to enter them again, let alone have fun there. Both the idea and skills that the painting demonstrates are excellent, but it is not qualified to gain awards for excellence in art production. After all, it is a plagiarized work. A foreign artist had created a painting in which an exhausted circus clown, sitting at a corner of the theater, watches audiences leaving after the end of the show. None of them, however, pay attention to the clown who has brought them so much laughter. Therefore, the first painting represents a form of plagiarism, rather than a legitimate borrowing of ideas.

### 陈文伯译文

> A literary or art work copied from others will be seen through no matter how skillfully it is disguised in a new form. That’s because you cannot assume to have the “soul” of the original. You think you’ve changed it into the soul of your work. But you can’t. It’s a pity that yours is not an original piece of work after all. Take a painting for an example. Just a glance at it can give some idea of its source. One can easily detect how it is delineated. The source from which the figure’s facial expression if copied can also be traced. The painting shows a migratory worker from farmland squatting at a corner of a city. He looks at people coming into and going out of luxurious places. Those people are depicted vividly, some big-bellied, some slender and coquettish, some radiant with joy and some full of pride and satisfaction. That worker is also vividly delineated: his expression on his face and in his eyes, his clothing and his bodily attitude are all true to life—showing the real living status of such a worker. Those magnificent houses are products of workers like him. It was they who had built them brick by brick and tile by tile until the construction work is finished by final decoration. That’s also time for him to go, he had no right to stay and enjoy the fruit of his labor. The idea to portray such a scene is praiseworthy, and the painting is also skillfully produced, but it is not qualified for a prize of creation. The reason is very simple: it is a product of plagiarism. A foreign painter had produced earlier a picture of a circus clown squatting wearily at a corner near the gate, looking at the audience leaving after the end of the show. They went away paying no attention at all to the clown who had caused them a lot of laughter. So we see what plagiarism is, it’s not what we call “draw on the achievement of others”.

## 批改反馈（Workbuddy · 2026-09-09）

**评级：5/10**（★5 文学材料，417 字；总评与 09-08 持平，但内部结构恶化：语言规范、地道度各降 1 分，靠完整度撑住）。
分项：忠实原文 5/10 ｜ 语言规范 4/10 ｜ 表达地道 4/10 ｜ 完整度 6/10。

### 值得保持的
- 「更别提」→ let alone 结构用对，且前后半句时态衔接自然。
- 「婀娜妖冶」→ alluring，与参考译文用词撞车，说明你主动词汇里有存货。
- 六段与原文一一对应，无整句漏译，叙述时态主线（现在时描述画作）基本稳住了。

### 错误清单（按严重度）

**A. 词义混淆/反转（最危险）**

| 原文 | 你的译文 | 问题 |
|---|---|---|
| 这就是"抄袭"，不是"借鉴" | That's plagrization, not **reference** | reference = 参考/引用，点题句关键词译错，收束全篇的一句没了说服力。→ plagiarism, not (a) borrowing / drawing inspiration |
| 眼神 | **eyesight** | eyesight = 视力（看得清不清），"眼神"是眼睛里的神情 → the look in his eyes / his eyes。sight 与 look 的混淆和 derive/deprive 同属一类高危错误 |

**B. 语法硬伤**

1. **双谓语结构**："These crowds, some of them **are** fat..., **are painted** lively" —— 插入语用了完整谓语 are，主句又 are painted，一句两个谓语打架。插入语去谓语化：some big-bellied, some seductively dressed...
2. **with 复合结构误加谓语**："with the facial expression, eyesight, outfits and posture **are** all described well" —— with + 名词 + 分词/形容词，不能再有 are。→ with his facial expression, the look in his eyes, his clothes and posture all realistically reproduced（或干脆拆成独立句，参考译文即如此）。
3. **定语从句缺谓语**："a clown..., **who squatting** at a corner" —— who was squatting 或直接 squatting（去掉 who）。
4. **comma splice**："...built by their hands, **however**, they didn't have..." —— however 是副词，不能逗号连接两个分句。→ "; however," 或拆两句。
5. "**squatting at there**" —— there 是副词，前面不能加 at。
6. "None of them **would pay** attention" —— would 误用，陈述事实：none paid / none pay attention。
7. **拼写：plagrized（×2）/ plagrization（×1）→ plagiarized / plagiarism**。本篇主题词全文拼错 3 次，考场上每出现一次就扣一次印象分，属于必须零失误的词。
8. 冠词：Take a painting as **an** example；a corner of **the** theatre；第 5 段小丑第二次提到应为 **the** clown。

**C. 用词不地道**

1. **春风满面 → confident**：词义错位。春风满面 = 满面笑容（all smiles / radiant with joy）；confident 偏"自信"，跑去了"踌躇满志"的方向。
2. **踌躇满志 → ambitious**：ambitious = 有野心的，原文是"自得意满"（enormously proud / full of pride and satisfaction）。两个四字格方向正好拧了。
3. "as you **put the sight on**"：Chinglish，"一看" = at a glance / at first sight。
4. "**can be realized as** excellent"：不通，直接 are excellent。
5. "the painting is **incapable**"：incapable 指"（人）无能力的"；"不够格" = not qualified (for) / doesn't deserve。
6. "**with tiredness**"：名词硬凑，exhausted（定语）或 wearily（状语）。
7. "大肚便便 → fat"：贬义过强且笼统，big-bellied。
8. "**figure out**"（×2）：figure out = 弄明白、想清楚；"看出来/识破" = detect / see through。
9. "哪一笔 → touches"：brushstrokes / strokes。
10. "painted **lively**"：lively 是形容词，副词 vividly / in a lively manner。

**D. 漏译**

1. 「蹲在**都市一隅**」—— 整片限定成分丢失（squatting at there 只剩一个错误结构，"都市""一隅"皆无）。→ squatting at a corner of a city。
2. 「望着某一奢侈场所**进进出出**的人」—— "进进出出"漏。→ people entering and leaving a luxury building。
3. 「**某一级别**的"**创作**奖"」—— 两个限定词全丢，some awards 过泛。→ awards for excellence in art production / a prize of creation。
4. 「**可惜**你不是」—— "可惜"的惋惜语气漏（However, you can't 只剩转折）。→ In fact / It's a pity, but you can't。
5. 「他就**再也**没有机会进去了」—— "再也"（never again / no longer）未译出，且"他"被换成 they，与前文 the rural worker 单复数断裂。
6. 「望着**散场**离去的人」—— "散场"（after the show）漏。

### 复盘要点

1. **高频主题词拼写扫描（30 秒流程）**：交卷前把全文出现 ≥2 次的实词过一遍拼写。本篇 plagiarism 拼错 3 次，是"一个字毁一篇"的典型——加入审计协议检查清单。
2. **"去谓语化"专练**：本次 3 处语法硬伤同出一源——在插入语、with 结构、定语从句里多写或少写了谓语动词。规则一句话：插入语和 with 结构里只放形容词/分词/介词短语，放完整句子必炸。找 10 个 with 复合结构例句仿写。
3. **四字格先拆义再选词**：春风满面/踌躇满志这类，先在心里翻成大白话（"满脸笑容"/"得意洋洋"），再从大白话选英文词，禁止凭字面联想（春风得意 → confident）直接落笔。

# 【南京师范大学MTI 2020】中外合作 天天快乐

今天是六一国际儿童节。

各位同学，节日快乐！

今天的材料来自于南京师范大学 2020 年的 MTI 汉英题目。这是一篇典型的政经类材料，内容比较多，难度实际上也并不算高。原因非常简单，里面充斥着太多基本概念，套路满满。

希望大家喜欢今天的这份节日礼物。

- **难度**：★★★★★

## 完整原文

中国推动构建公正、合理、透明的国际经贸投资规则体系，促进生产要素有序流动、资源高效配置、市场深度融合。

中国积极吸引外国机构和个人来华投资兴业，外商直接投资规模从 1992 年起连续 26 年居发展中国家首位。加入世贸组织后，外商直接投资规模从 2001 年的 468.8 亿美元增加到 2017 年的 1363.2 亿美元，年均增长 6.9%。外商投资企业在提升中国经济增长质量和效益的同时，分享中国经济发展红利。中国美国商会《2018 中国商务环境调查报告》显示，约 60% 的受访企业将中国列为全球三大投资目的地之一，74% 的会员企业计划于 2018 年扩大在华投资，这一比例为近年来最高，其中三分之一的受访企业计划增加在华投资 10% 以上。中国欧盟商会《商业信心调查 2018》报告显示，超过一半的会员企业计划扩大在华运营规模。2017 年全国新设立外商投资企业 35652 家，同比增长 27.8%。

中国对外投资合作持续健康规范发展，对外直接投资年度流量全球排名从加入世贸组织之初的第 26 位上升至 2017 年的第 3 位。中国对外投资合作加快了东道国当地技术进步步伐，促进其经济发展和民生改善，创造了大量就业机会。

当今世界正在经历新一轮大发展大变革大调整，人类面临的不稳定不确定因素依然很多。近年来，逆全球化潮流涌动，保护主义和单边主义抬头，以世贸组织为核心的多边贸易体制遭遇挑战。

经济全球化为世界经济增长提供了强劲动力，是不可逆转的时代潮流，中国与多边贸易体制休戚与共。中国将继续践行承诺、遵守规则，积极参与多边贸易体制建设，坚定支持世贸组织在全球经济治理中发挥更大作用。

## 句式精析

### 1. 中国推动构建公正、合理、透明的国际经贸投资规则体系，促进生产要素有序流动、资源高效配置、市场深度融合。

**【参考译文】** China promotes the establishment of fair, equitable and transparent system of rules for international trade and investment, with the aim to facilitate the orderly flow of production factors, the efficient allocation of resources, and the deeper integration of domestic and international markets.

**【官方译文】** China has been promoting the establishment of a fair, equitable and transparent system of international trade and investment rules to boost the orderly flow of production factors, efficient resources allocation and full market integration.

「推动构建」是两个并列的动词，翻译起来有不同的处理思路。比如 promote the establishment of。简单一点就是 work to build。

「公正、合理、透明的」这三个定语已经是政经类材料里面的熟客了，一定得会。「国际经贸投资规则体系」同样也是基本概念，得会。

「生产要素」眼熟吗？「高效配置」同样都是基本概念。「市场高度融合」看起来非常简单，但是你要注意：融合的宾语起码是多个东西，一个市场是无法融合的。所以，这个地方的「市场」应该是「国内市场」与「国际市场」。

---

### 2. 中国积极吸引外国机构和个人来华投资兴业，外商直接投资规模从 1992 年起连续 26 年居发展中国家首位。

**【参考译文】** China makes robust efforts to attract investment from international organizations and individuals. Since 1992, it has been the largest FDI recipient in the developing world for 26 years on end.

**【官方译文】** China has proactively attracted foreign institutions and individuals to invest and develop in China. Since 1992, China has consistently topped the list of FDI recipients among developing countries for 26 years consecutively.

「积极」出现，是不是把它翻译成 actively？表达是成立的，但不如换一些更直爽的表达，比如说 make robust efforts。

「投资兴业」看起来是两层含义，但实际上就是投资。为啥？看看后面的内容你就知道了。中文为了凑四个字的表达结构，往往含义上「添油加醋」。

「外商直接投资规模」一定不要写成了 FBI，而是 FDI。而且，此处的「规模」二字可以省略；如果非要直译，可以考虑用 amount 或者 inflows。

「居发展中国家首位」的表达方式，那实在是太多了。比如，用最高级或者用比较级；或者，用强势动词 lead 或者 rank。

只不过，后半句的逻辑主语是什么？形式上来讲是「规模」，但实际上呢？还是「中国」。

---

### 3. 加入世贸组织后，外商直接投资规模从 2001 年的 468.8 亿美元增加到 2017 年的 1363.2 亿美元，年均增长 6.9%。

**【参考译文】** After its accession to the World Trade Organization in 2001, its FDI inflows increased from 46.88 billion dollars to 136.32 billion dollars in 2017, representing an annual increase of 6.9%.

**【官方译文】** After China's accession to the WTO, its FDI increased from USD46.88 billion in 2001 to USD136.32 billion in 2017, up by an annual average of 6.9 percent.

「世贸组织」都得会，千万不要写成 WHO。而且，还要注意 WTO 前面的定冠词要不要加？

「外商直接投资规模」再次出现，可以想办法化解一下，避免无所谓的措辞重复。「年均增长 6.9%」从意群上来讲，属于补充说明，考虑使用从句结构。

此处，数字千万不要出现错误，比如说把 billion 翻译成 million。

---

### 4. 外商投资企业在提升中国经济增长质量和效益的同时，分享中国经济发展红利。

**【参考译文】** In addition to boosting the quality and efficiency of the Chinese economy, foreign-investment enterprises have shared the dividends delivered by its development.

**【官方译文】** FIEs have shared the benefits of China's economic development, while helping improve the quality and performance of China's economy.

「外商投资企业」同样是固定概念，不得不会。「质量和效益」怎么处理呢？前者是 quality。后者是 effect 吗？感觉不像。不如 efficiency 更好一些。

「分享中国经济发展红利」没啥可说的吧？这都是基本套话了。还要注意的是，「中国经济」有些重复，要想办法化解。

---

### 5. 中国美国商会《2018 中国商务环境调查报告》显示，约 60% 的受访企业将中国列为全球三大投资目的地之一，74% 的会员企业计划于 2018 年扩大在华投资，这一比例为近年来最高，其中三分之一的受访企业计划增加在华投资 10% 以上。

**【参考译文】** According to the 2018 China Business Climate Survey Report by the American Chamber of Commerce in China, about 60% of the surveyed companies list China among their top three investment destinations, 74% of member enterprises plan to expand investment in China in 2018, the highest percentage over recent years. Notably, 1/3 of the surveyed companies plan to increase investment in the country by over 10%.

**【官方译文】** According to the "2018 China Business Climate Survey Report" by the American Chamber of Commerce in China (AmCham China), nearly 60 percent of the interviewed enterprises ranked China as a top three investment priority; some 74 percent of the AmCham China member enterprises plan to expand their investments in China in 2018, the highest in recent years, and one third of the interviewed enterprises plan to increase their investments in China by over 10 percent.

本句的内容明显有些多，要考虑适当的断句。

「中国美国商会」官方机构，肯定是有自己英文名字。考场上写错的话，其实能理解。这并不是考试的重点或者难点。

「2018 中国商务环境调查报告」像这种报告的名字，不知道的话怎么办？别着急，咱们直接使用模糊大法。比如说 a report。

呆毛，英文里面有没有书名号？凡是在英文写作或者翻译里面，出现顿号或者书名号的同学，都应该报个幼儿园，复读三年。

「这一比例为近年来最高」明显是修饰前面的 74%，直接用同位语结构就可以。

---

### 6. 中国欧盟商会《商业信心调查 2018》报告显示，超过一半的会员企业计划扩大在华运营规模。

**【参考译文】** According to the Business Confidence Survey 2018 by the European Union Chamber of Commerce in China, more than half of its member companies plan to expand their operations in China.

**【官方译文】** According to the "Business Confidence Survey 2018" by the European Union Chamber of Commerce in China, more than half of its member enterprises plan to expand their presence in China.

「中国欧盟商会《商业信心调查 2018》」既有官方名称，也有报告名字，同样不是考试的重点。只不过从翻译的角度来讲，这些概念必须平时多加积累。

「运营规模」一个单词就可以，operations。对，是个复数形态。

---

### 7. 2017 年全国新设立外商投资企业 35652 家，同比增长 27.8%。

**【参考译文】** In 2017, 35,652 FIEs were established across the nation, up by 27.8% year on year.

**【官方译文】** In 2017, newly founded FIEs in China reached 35,652, registering an increase of 27.8 percent.

这句话看起来非常的简单，但是一定要注意主干搭配是否成立。

「全国设立外商投资企业」看起来人畜无害的主干搭配，成立吗？想好了再说。否则幼儿园报名都来不及了。

后面的「同比增长 27.8%」，这个句式结构在以前的月度测试里面经常出现，必须得会。

---

### 8. 中国对外投资合作持续健康规范发展，对外直接投资年度流量全球排名从加入世贸组织之初的第 26 位上升至 2017 年的第 3 位。

**【参考译文】** At the same time, China's outbound investment has witnessed sustained and healthy growth, with its global ranking in terms of FDI outflows increasing from No 26 in 2001 to No 3 in 2017.

**【官方译文】** China's outward investment cooperation has developed in a sustained, sound, and orderly way. In terms of annual flow of outward direct investment (ODI), China's world ranking rose from the 26th place after its accession to the WTO to the third in 2017.

这句话的内容不多，但是逻辑上要注意，是一个典型的总分关系。后半句可以处理成一个伴随从句。

「对外投资合作」里面的「合作」赶紧删除，为啥呢？因为没有实际意义，就是讲的中国的对外投资。

「持续健康规范发展」前面的三个定语如何处理？感觉「规范」二字可以不翻译，为啥呢？因为「健康」就能够体现「规范」。毕竟，不健康的话肯定因为不规范。

「对外直接投资年度流量」当你看到「流量」的时候，可不要用什么 traffic。此处，其实用 amount 或者 outflows。

---

### 9. 中国对外投资合作加快了东道国当地技术进步步伐，促进其经济发展和民生改善，创造了大量就业机会。

**【参考译文】** Its investment has facilitated technological progress of recipient countries, contributed to local economic development and livelihood improvement, and created massive job opportunities.

**【官方译文】** China's outward investment cooperation has accelerated technological progress in the host countries, advanced their economic development, improved their people's well-being and created many jobs.

「中国对外投资合作」又在重复了，怎么办呢？开动你的大脑，思考吧。

「东道国」是什么样的国家？其实就是中国对外投资的接受国。英文叫 recipient countries。根据不同的语境，有时候也可以翻译成「受援国」。

「步伐」二字没有什么实际意义，直接删除。「经济发展和民生改善」都是最基本的套话了，不得不会。后面的内容同样如此。

---

### 10. 当今世界正在经历新一轮大发展大变革大调整，人类面临的不稳定不确定因素依然很多。

**【参考译文】** The world today is undergoing a new round of greater development, dramatic changes, and profound transformation. Humanity is still confronted with many destabilizing and uncertain factors.

**【官方译文】** The world is undergoing a new round of major development, great change and profound readjustment. The mankind still faces growing uncertainties and destabilizing factors.

这句话有啥可讲的吗？真的就是套话中的套话了。以前也在公众号的材料里面讲解过。

### 11. 近年来，逆全球化潮流涌动，保护主义和单边主义抬头，以世贸组织为核心的多边贸易体制遭遇挑战。

**【参考译文】** For example, the growing backlash against globalization and the rise of protectionism and unilateralism have posed a challenge to the WTO-centered multilateral trading system.

**【官方译文】** Surging tides of anti-globalization in recent years, coupled with rising protectionism and unilateralism, have posed severe challenges to the multilateral trading system with the WTO at its core.

「涌动」是啥？就是「不断发展」。「保护主义和单边主义抬头」，像这种套话，小学生一分钱能给你唱 10 分钟。

「以世贸组织为核心的多边贸易体制」，给大学生一分钱，他能给你唱三小时。

只不过，这句话的内部逻辑关系需要大家考虑一下。看起来是三个独立的意群，但实际上呢？考虑一下：遭遇挑战的原因是什么？或者说，这些挑战指的是什么？

### 12. 经济全球化为世界经济增长提供了强劲动力，是不可逆转的时代潮流，中国与多边贸易体制休戚与共。

**【参考译文】** Economic globalization has provided a robust growth driver for the world economy and is an irreversible trend of the times. China has a huge stake in the multilateral trading system.

**【官方译文】** Economic globalization has powered global growth and is an irreversible trend of our times. China and the multilateral trading system stand together through thick and thin.

「提供了强劲动力」没啥可说的吧？「不可逆转的时代潮流」同样也是套话中的套套套话，必须得会。

「休戚与共」可能有同学立马想到了 share weal and woe。呆毛，总感觉这个表达用在这个地方有点怪。我想到的表达是 have a high stake in。有没有见过？

### 参考译文

China promotes the establishment of fair, equitable and transparent system of rules for international trade and investment, with the aim to facilitate the orderly flow of production factors, the efficient allocation of resources, and the deeper integration of domestic and international markets.

China makes robust efforts to attract investment from international organizations and individuals. Since 1992, it has been the largest FDI recipient in the developing world for 26 years on end. After its accession to the World Trade Organization in 2001, its FDI inflows increased from 46.88 billion dollars to 136.32 billion dollars in 2017, representing an annual increase of 6.9%. In addition to boosting the quality and efficiency of the Chinese economy, foreign-investment enterprises have shared the dividends delivered by its development. According to the 2018 China Business Climate Survey Report by the American Chamber of Commerce in China, about 60% of the surveyed companies list China among their top three investment destinations, 74% of member enterprises plan to expand investment in China in 2018, the highest percentage over recent years. Notably, 1/3 of the surveyed companies plan to increase investment in the country by over 10%. According to the Business Confidence Survey 2018 by the European Union Chamber of Commerce in China, more than half of its member companies plan to expand their operations in China. In 2017, 35,652 FIEs were established across the nation, up by 27.8% year on year.

At the same time, China's outbound investment has witnessed sustained and healthy growth, with its global ranking in terms of FDI outflows increasing from No 26 in 2001 to No 3 in 2017. Its investment has facilitated technological progress of recipient countries, contributed to local economic development and livelihood improvement, and created massive job opportunities.

The world today is undergoing a new round of greater development, dramatic changes, and profound transformation. Humanity is still confronted with many destabilizing and uncertain factors. For example, the growing backlash against globalization and the rise of protectionism and unilateralism have posed a challenge to the WTO-centered multilateral trading system.

Economic globalization has provided a robust growth driver for the world economy and is an irreversible trend of the times. China has a huge stake in the multilateral trading system. Going forward, it will continue to honor its commitments and abide by rules, contribute to the development of the multilateral trading system, and firmly support the WTO in playing a greater role in global economic governance.

### 官方译文

China has been promoting the establishment of a fair, equitable and transparent system of international trade and investment rules to boost the orderly flow of production factors, efficient resources allocation and full market integration.

China has proactively attracted foreign institutions and individuals to invest and develop in China. Since 1992, China has consistently topped the list of FDI recipients among developing countries for 26 years consecutively. After China's accession to the WTO, its FDI increased from USD46.88 billion in 2001 to USD136.32 billion in 2017, up by an annual average of 6.9 percent. FIEs have shared the benefits of China's economic development, while helping improve the quality and performance of China's economy. According to the "2018 China Business Climate Survey Report" by the American Chamber of Commerce in China (AmCham China), nearly 60 percent of the interviewed enterprises ranked China as a top three investment priority; some 74 percent of the AmCham China member enterprises plan to expand their investments in China in 2018, the highest in recent years, and one third of the interviewed enterprises plan to increase their investments in China by over 10 percent. According to the "Business Confidence Survey 2018" by the European Union Chamber of Commerce in China, more than half of its member enterprises plan to expand their presence in China. In 2017, newly founded FIEs in China reached 35,652, registering an increase of 27.8 percent.

China's outward investment cooperation has developed in a sustained, sound, and orderly way. In terms of annual flow of outward direct investment (ODI), China's world ranking rose from the 26th place after its accession to the WTO to the third in 2017. China's outward investment cooperation has accelerated technological progress in the host countries, advanced their economic development, improved their people's well-being and created many jobs.

The world is undergoing a new round of major development, great change and profound readjustment. The mankind still faces growing uncertainties and destabilizing factors. Surging tides of anti-globalization in recent years, coupled with rising protectionism and unilateralism, have posed severe challenges to the multilateral trading system with the WTO at its core.

Economic globalization has powered global growth and is an irreversible trend of our times. China and the multilateral trading system stand together through thick and thin. China will continue to fulfill its commitments, comply with rules, actively participate in the improvement of the multilateral trading system, and give firm support to the WTO in playing a greater role in global economic governance.

## 重点表达积累

- **推动构建**：promote the establishment of / work to build / be promoting the establishment of

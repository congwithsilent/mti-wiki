# 【新疆大学MTI 2018】你好，世界。

结束了，一切都结束了。

国庆假期已经成为回忆，下一个假期在 2027 年。不知道在刚刚结束的这个假期里，大家游览了各地的大好河山，还是待在学校里面，继续奋笔疾书。

现在是 10 月初，距离考研满打满算，是两个半月的时间。看起来不多，但仍然可以做很多事情。只不过，从这现在开始，不少同学内心的压力感会越来越强烈。

说到压力，大家想想昨天爆发的一条新闻。某款汽车的刹车踏板，竟然在测试时被踩断了。而且不是一辆同款汽车，是三辆。开车这么多年，也是第一次听说这样的事情。毕竟，经常遭人唾弃的老头乐的刹车踏板，也没听说过谁能踩断。

新闻一出，背后生产公司的市值暴跌 60 亿。这是啥概念呢？从盘古开天辟地到现在，我也挣不了那么多钱呀。

今天的材料，来自新疆大学 2018 年的 MTI 汉英翻译题目。这篇材料内容不多，难度也不高，属于典型的政经类材料。

以前，有同学给我发来过一篇这所学校的汉英翻译题目，内容是关于思政教育的。那篇材料，通篇都是抽象晦涩的措辞，根本没有翻译成英文的价值。为啥呢？内宣跟外宣是两码事。换句话说，并非所有的中文材料都有翻译成外文的意义和价值。

部分人觉得，翻译无非就是把中文换成另外一种语言。但背后牵扯的因素多种多样。除了语言，还有价值观、文化背景、历史背景，甚至宗教信仰等。单纯在字面转换，有时候适得其反。

这么多年，我也发现了一个非常有意思的现象：新疆地区的同学比较少。不管是汉族、维族，还是当地的少数民族，是不是学英语的比较少？或者说，考 MTI 的学生群体比较少？

- **难度**：★★★★★
- **字数**：271 字
- **建议翻译时间**：30 - 40 分钟
- **翻译难点**：政治、外交类概念的表达

## 完整原文

外长们承诺继续共同坚持互尊互谅、平等相待、团结互助、开放包容、互惠互利，进一步深化战略伙伴关系，深化金砖务实合作，以充实第二个“金色十年”团结合作的实际内涵。

外长们就国际和地区政治经济问题交换意见，认为世界经济正在复苏，同时下行风险和不确定性依然普遍存在。他们注意到金砖国家仍在扮演全球增长引擎的重要角色，重申应促进世界经济增长，包括通过加强宏观经济政策协调、改善全球经济治理等。

外长们重申对联合国的支持。联合国作为具有普遍代表性的多边组织，肩负维护国际和平与安全，推动全球发展、促进和保护人权的授权，旨在为人类命运共同体开辟更加光明的未来。

## 句式精析

### 1. 外长们承诺继续共同坚持互尊互谅、平等相待、团结互助、开放包容、互惠互利，进一步深化战略伙伴关系，深化金砖务实合作，以充实第二个“金色十年”团结合作的实际内涵。

**【参考译文】** The foreign ministers pledged to continue to collectively uphold mutual respect and mutual understanding, equality, solidarity, openness, inclusiveness and mutually beneficial cooperation, to deepen strategic partnership and enhance results-oriented cooperation and ultimately to enrich the substance of the second golden decade of BRICS cooperation and solidarity.

**【官方译文】** They further pledged to continue working together to uphold mutual respect, equality, solidarity, openness and inclusiveness, to further strengthen strategic partnership cooperation for mutual benefit by constantly deepening BRICS practical cooperation so as to usher in and provide practical content to the second golden decade of BRICS cooperation and solidarity.

本句的内容非常多，但是可以不断句，因为原文的逻辑关系比较密切。当然，如果表达能力有限，可以考虑适当断句。比如，最后的“以”字明显表示目的，可以考虑处理成独立的一句话。

“外长”译错的同学，以后当不成外交部长，可千万别怪我现在没提醒你好好学习。“坚持”可以考虑简单的动词 uphold 或者 stay committed to。处理成 persist in 或者 insist on 的同学，有没有留下不学无术的泪水？

“互尊互谅、平等相待、团结互助、开放包容、互惠互利”在翻译的时候，一定要注意表达结构的对仗和统一。如果想使用名词结构，那这五个结构都是名词。不要一会用名词，一会用动词 ing，甚至一会儿又想起来用句子。

像这种套话，多积累一些就可以。此处，其实指的是“金砖精神”的内涵。

“进一步”这三个字可翻可不翻，因为它的本质含义其实没有，只不过是一种强调态度。

“金砖”在形式上是定语，但实际上是状语。表示：在金砖体制内。至于“务实合作”，本身是常见的外交辞令：results-oriented cooperation。

“金色十年”这种概念肯定是当年的新闻热点话题，得积累。“实际内涵”是啥意思？有个比较常用的抽象名词是 substance。

---

### 2. 外长们就国际和地区政治经济问题交换意见，认为世界经济正在复苏，同时下行风险和不确定性依然普遍存在。

**【参考译文】** The ministers exchanged views on international and regional issues in political and economic sectors, and recognized that the world economy is recovering while downward risks persist globally.

**【官方译文】** The Ministers exchanged views on global and regional issues in the economic and political spheres and recognized that global economic recovery is gaining momentum though uncertainties and downside risks persist globally.

“交换意见”表达特别多，完全可以直译，比如说 exchange views。

“认为”用 think 的话，显得比较 low，太口语化。“世界经济正在复苏”没啥可讲。“下行风险”同样也是套话。

“依然存在”表达特别多，能想到三个动词吗？比如说 persist, remain 或者 linger。

---

### 3. 他们注意到金砖国家仍在扮演全球增长引擎的重要角色，重申应促进世界经济增长，包括通过加强宏观经济政策协调、改善全球经济治理等。

**【参考译文】** They noted that BRICS countries continue to play an important role as engines of global growth, and reiterated the need to drive global economic growth by strengthening macro-economic policy coordination and upgrading global economic governance.

**【官方译文】** They noted that BRICS countries continue to play an important role as engines of global growth. They further reiterated the need to boost world economic growth including through macro-economic policy coordination and improving global economic governance.

“注意到”可以考虑用 note。“重申”是 reaffirm 吗？看后面的内容以及这个单词的含义，明显不恰当。此处其实是 Reiterate。

“应促进”的逻辑主语是什么？此处没有明说，但全句的含义暗示了，能发现吗？

“宏观经济政策协调”和“改善全球经济治理”统统都是套话，没啥可讲的。

---

### 4. 外长们重申对联合国的支持。

**【参考译文】** The ministers reiterated their support to the United Nations.

**【官方译文】** The Ministers reaffirmed their commitment to the United Nations...

没啥可说的吧？

但是，此处有没有同学把联合国翻译成 the US？如果是你的话，哪凉快哪呆着去。

---

### 5. 联合国作为具有普遍代表性的多边组织，肩负维护国际和平与安全，推动全球发展、促进和保护人权的授权，旨在为人类命运共同体开辟更加光明的未来。

**【参考译文】** The UN, as the universal multilateral organization, is mandated to maintain world peace and security, promote global development, and enhance and protect human rights, so as to build a brighter future for a global community with a shared future.

**【官方译文】** ...as the universal multilateral organization entrusted with the mandate for maintaining international peace and security, advance global development and to promote and protect human rights so as to build a brighter shared future for the global community.

“普遍代表性”其实也是一个套话，universal。“授权”可以考虑 mandate。“维护国际和平与安全，推动全球发展、促进和保护人权”都是套话。

“人类命运共同体”同样是比较常见的概念，必须得会。而且此时需要注意一个很小的细节。以前的人类往往会翻译成 mankind，现在比较推荐的是 humanity。为啥呢？性别平等。

“开辟”简单一个动词就是 build。

外长们承诺继续共同坚持互尊互谅、平等相待、团结互助、开放包容、互惠互利，进一步深化战略伙伴关系，深化金砖务实合作，以充实第二个“金色十年”团结合作的实际内涵。

外长们就国际和地区政治经济问题交换意见，认为世界经济正在复苏，同时下行风险和不确定性依然普遍存在。他们注意到金砖国家仍在扮演全球增长引擎的重要角色，重申应促进世界经济增长，包括通过加强宏观经济政策协调、改善全球经济治理等。

外长们重申对联合国的支持。联合国作为具有普遍代表性的多边组织，肩负维护国际和平与安全，推动全球发展、促进和保护人权的授权，旨在为人类命运共同体开辟更加光明的未来。

### 参考译文

The foreign ministers pledged to continue to collectively uphold mutual respect and mutual understanding, equality, solidarity, openness, inclusiveness and mutually beneficial cooperation, to deepen strategic partnership and enhance results-oriented cooperation and ultimately to enrich the substance of the second golden decade of BRICS cooperation and solidarity.

The ministers exchanged views on international and regional issues in political and economic sectors, and recognized that the world economy is recovering while downward risks persist globally. They noted that BRICS countries continue to play an important role as engines of global growth, and reiterated the need to drive global economic growth by strengthening macro-economic policy coordination and upgrading global economic governance.

The ministers reiterated their support to the United Nations. The UN, as the universal multilateral organization, is mandated to maintain world peace and security, promote global development, and enhance and protect human rights, so as to build a brighter future for a global community with a shared future.

### 官方译文

They further pledged to continue working together to uphold mutual respect, equality, solidarity, openness and inclusiveness, to further strengthen strategic partnership cooperation for mutual benefit by constantly deepening BRICS practical cooperation so as to usher in and provide practical content to the second golden decade of BRICS cooperation and solidarity.

The Ministers exchanged views on global and regional issues in the economic and political spheres and recognized that global economic recovery is gaining momentum though uncertainties and downside risks persist globally. They noted that BRICS countries continue to play an important role as engines of global growth. They further reiterated the need to boost world economic growth including through macro-economic policy coordination and improving global economic governance.

The Ministers reaffirmed their commitment to the United Nations as the universal multilateral organization entrusted with the mandate for maintaining international peace and security, advance global development and to promote and protect human rights so as to build a brighter shared future for the global community.

## 重点表达积累

- **外长**：foreign minister / the ministers（复数时须加 the）
- **坚持（承诺继续坚持）**：uphold / stay committed to（避坑：persist in / insist on）
- **互尊互谅**：mutual respect and mutual understanding / mutual respect
- **平等相待**：equality
- **团结互助**：solidarity
- **开放包容**：openness and inclusiveness / openness, inclusiveness
- **互惠互利**：mutually beneficial cooperation / mutual benefit
- **战略伙伴关系**：strategic partnership / strategic partnership cooperation
- **务实合作**：results-oriented cooperation / practical cooperation
- **金色十年**：the golden decade
- **实际内涵**：substance / practical content
- **交换意见**：exchange views
- **下行风险**：downward risks / downside risks
- **依然存在**：persist / remain / linger
- **注意到**：note
- **重申**：reiterate（避坑：reaffirm）
- **全球增长引擎**：engines of global growth
- **宏观经济政策协调**：macro-economic policy coordination
- **改善全球经济治理**：upgrade / improve global economic governance
- **普遍代表性**：universal
- **授权**：mandate
- **人类命运共同体**：a global community with a shared future
- **人类**：humanity（避坑：mankind，出于性别平等）
- **开辟（更光明的未来）**：build

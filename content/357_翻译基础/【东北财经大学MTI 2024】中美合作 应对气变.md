## 完整原文

中美致力于相互合作并与其他国家一道解决气候危机，按其严峻性、紧迫性所要求加以应对。这既包括强化各自行动，也包括在联合国气候变化框架公约和巴黎协定等多边进程中开展合作。双方回顾两国气候变化领域的领导力与合作，为巴黎协定的制定、通过、签署和生效作出历史性贡献。

走向未来，中美两国坚持携手并与其他各方一道加强巴黎协定的实施。双方回顾巴黎协定第二条的目的在于将全球平均气温上升控制在低于2℃之内，并努力限制在1.5℃之内。为此，双方承诺继续作出努力，包括在巴黎协定框架下21世纪20年代采取提高力度的强化行动，以使上述温升限制目标可以实现，并合作识别和应对相关挑战与机遇。

两国计划采取适当行动，尽可能扩大国际投融资支持发展中国家从高碳化石能源向绿色、低碳和可再生能源转型。

## 句式精析

### 1. 中美致力于相互合作并与其他国家一道解决气候危机，按其严峻性、紧迫性所要求加以应对。

**【参考译文】** China and the United States are committed to working together and with other countries to address the climate crisis, and to taking action in light of its severity and urgency.

**【官方译文】** China and the United States are committed to cooperating with each other and with other countries to tackle the climate crisis, which must be addressed with the seriousness and urgency that it demands.

“中美”出现，不要把美国翻译成 America。在严肃的政治场合，这是极不恰当的表达。

“致力于”表达非常丰富。正式一些的 be committed to。简单的 work to，strive to 或者 endeavor to。那么，“相互合作”表达有问题吗？

明眼人一看就知道，典型的语义重复。

“按”要理解它的准确含义，也就是“鉴于，基于”。可以考虑 in light of。

---

### 2. 这既包括强化各自行动，也包括在联合国气候变化框架公约和巴黎协定等多边进程中开展合作。

**【参考译文】** This includes enhancing their respective efforts and engaging in cooperation within multilateral processes, including the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Paris Agreement.

**【官方译文】** This includes both enhancing their respective actions and cooperating in multilateral processes, including the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Paris Agreement.

“包括”你是不是想到了用 include？没问题的，也可以考虑动词 mean 或者 entail 或者 require。

“各自行动”就是“自己的行动”，直译成英文叫 respective action。“联合国气候变化框架公约和巴黎协定”肯定有现成的英文版本。自己编的话，凶多吉少。

---

### 3. 双方回顾两国气候变化领域的领导力与合作，为巴黎协定的制定、通过、签署和生效作出历史性贡献。

**【参考译文】** Both countries review their historic contribution to the development, adoption, signature, and entry into force of the Paris Agreement through their leadership and collaboration.

**【官方译文】** Both countries recall their historic contribution to the development, adoption, signature, and entry into force of the Paris Agreement through their leadership and collaboration.

“回顾”，一个 r 开头的动词。“领导力与合作”也都是基本的词汇。“制定、通过、签署和生效”这 4 个名词都要翻译出来吗？一定的，为啥？

这是一种特意的强调。

只不过，此处有一个理解上的黑洞。“回顾”的宾语在形式上来讲，应该是“领导力和合作”。但实际上呢？官方版本里的宾语是“历史性贡献”！

理解出现偏差怎么办？怪原文作者写得不好，逻辑层次不清楚，容易造成误解。

其实，只要把原文“合作”后面的逗号去掉就可以了。

---

### 4. 走向未来，中美两国坚持携手并与其他各方一道加强巴黎协定的实施。

**【参考译文】** Going forward, China and the United States will continue to work together and with other Parties to facilitate the implementation of the Paris Agreement.

**【官方译文】** Moving forward, China and the United States are firmly committed to working together and with other Parties to strengthen implementation of the Paris Agreement.

“走向”是一定 walk to 吗？这个动词其实没啥实际意义，直接处理成介词 in 就可以。当然，这四个字的整体含义，可以处理成 going forward，in the days ahead 之类的。

“坚持”出现，肯定还有人把它翻译成 persist in 或者 insist on。说的是你吗，大聪明？

后面的内容就没啥可讲的了，都是套话。

---

### 5. 双方回顾巴黎协定第二条的目的在于将全球平均气温上升控制在低于2℃之内，并努力限制在1.5℃之内。

**【参考译文】** The two sides reaffirm the Agreement's goal, outlined in Article 2, of "holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels and pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels."

**【官方译文】** The two sides recall the Agreement's aim in accordance with Article 2 to hold the global average temperature increase to well below 2 degrees C and to pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5 degrees C.

这句话的内容，看起来有点长，但实际上是套话。这也是巴黎协定最重要的目标。

---

### 6. 为此，双方承诺继续作出努力，包括在巴黎协定框架下21世纪20年代采取提高力度的强化行动，以使上述温升限制目标可以实现，并合作识别和应对相关挑战与机遇。

**【参考译文】** They have pledged to make such efforts, including taking enhanced climate actions that raise ambition in the 2020s within the framework of the Paris Agreement with the aim of keeping these temperature targets within reach, and cooperating to identify and address related challenges and opportunities.

**【官方译文】** In that regard, they are committed to pursuing such efforts, including by taking enhanced climate actions that raise ambition in the 2020s in the context of the Paris Agreement with the aim of keeping the above temperature limit within reach and cooperating to identify and address related challenges and opportunities.

“作出努力”你是不是想到了 make effort？“采取提高力度的强化行动”，你听听这中文，是人话吗？别扭得耳朵都难受。

“使”出现，是用 make 吗？少用这个单词，求求了。“识别”直接对应英文单词 identify。

---

### 7. 两国计划采取适当行动，尽可能扩大国际投融资支持发展中国家从高碳化石能源向绿色、低碳和可再生能源转型。

**【参考译文】** The two countries stand ready to take appropriate actions to maximize international investment and finance in support of the transition from carbon-intensive fossil fuels to green, low-carbon and renewable energy in developing countries.

**【官方译文】** Both countries intend to take appropriate actions to maximize international investment and finance in support of the transition from carbon-intensive fossil fuel-based energy to green, low-carbon and renewable energy in developing countries.

“适当”可以考虑 proper 或者 appropriate。“投融资”算是一个固定概念，investment and finance。

“高碳化石能源”一看就是典型的英文结构。至于后面的“绿色、低碳和可再生能源转型”，有啥难度吗？

似乎没有。

## 原文与参考译文汇总

### 原文

中美致力于相互合作并与其他国家一道解决气候危机，按其严峻性、紧迫性所要求加以应对。这既包括强化各自行动，也包括在联合国气候变化框架公约和巴黎协定等多边进程中开展合作。双方回顾两国气候变化领域的领导力与合作，为巴黎协定的制定、通过、签署和生效作出历史性贡献。

走向未来，中美两国坚持携手并与其他各方一道加强巴黎协定的实施。双方回顾巴黎协定第二条的目的在于将全球平均气温上升控制在低于2℃之内，并努力限制在1.5℃之内。为此，双方承诺继续作出努力，包括在巴黎协定框架下21世纪20年代采取提高力度的强化行动，以使上述温升限制目标可以实现，并合作识别和应对相关挑战与机遇。

两国计划采取适当行动，尽可能扩大国际投融资支持发展中国家从高碳化石能源向绿色、低碳和可再生能源转型。

### 参考译文

China and the United States are committed to working together and with other countries to address the climate crisis, and to taking action in light of its severity and urgency. This includes enhancing their respective efforts and engaging in cooperation within multilateral processes, including the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Paris Agreement. Both countries review their historic contribution to the development, adoption, signature, and entry into force of the Paris Agreement through their leadership and collaboration.

Going forward, China and the United States will continue to work together and with other Parties to facilitate the implementation of the Paris Agreement. The two sides reaffirm the Agreement's goal, outlined in Article 2, of "holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels and pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels." They have pledged to make such efforts, including taking enhanced climate actions that raise ambition in the 2020s within the framework of the Paris Agreement with the aim of keeping these temperature targets within reach, and cooperating to identify and address related challenges and opportunities.

The two countries stand ready to take appropriate actions to maximize international investment and finance in support of the transition from carbon-intensive fossil fuels to green, low-carbon and renewable energy in developing countries.

### 官方译文

China and the United States are committed to cooperating with each other and with other countries to tackle the climate crisis, which must be addressed with the seriousness and urgency that it demands. This includes both enhancing their respective actions and cooperating in multilateral processes, including the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Paris Agreement. Both countries recall their historic contribution to the development, adoption, signature, and entry into force of the Paris Agreement through their leadership and collaboration.

Moving forward, China and the United States are firmly committed to working together and with other Parties to strengthen implementation of the Paris Agreement. The two sides recall the Agreement's aim in accordance with Article 2 to hold the global average temperature increase to well below 2 degrees C and to pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5 degrees C. In that regard, they are committed to pursuing such efforts, including by taking enhanced climate actions that raise ambition in the 2020s in the context of the Paris Agreement with the aim of keeping the above temperature limit within reach and cooperating to identify and address related challenges and opportunities.

Both countries intend to take appropriate actions to maximize international investment and finance in support of the transition from carbon-intensive fossil fuel-based energy to green, low-carbon and renewable energy in developing countries.

## 重点表达积累

- **中美**：China and the United States（政治场合勿用 America）
- **致力于**：be committed to / work to / strive to / endeavor to
- **相互合作**：语义重复，不必逐字对应
- **按（鉴于，基于）**：in light of
- **包括**：include / mean / entail / require
- **各自行动**：respective action / respective efforts
- **联合国气候变化框架公约**：the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change
- **巴黎协定**：the Paris Agreement
- **回顾**：recall / review / reaffirm
- **制定、通过、签署和生效**：development, adoption, signature, and entry into force
- **走向未来**：going forward / moving forward / in the days ahead / in
- **坚持（携手）**：continue to / be firmly committed to（勿用 persist in / insist on）
- **加强……实施**：facilitate the implementation of / strengthen implementation of
- **作出努力**：make efforts / pledge to make such efforts
- **提高力度的强化行动**：enhanced climate actions that raise ambition
- **识别和应对**：identify and address
- **使（避免滥用 make）**：with the aim of
- **适当**：proper / appropriate
- **投融资**：investment and finance
- **高碳化石能源**：carbon-intensive fossil fuels / carbon-intensive fossil fuel-based energy
- **尽可能扩大**：maximize
- **支持**：in support of
- **转型**：transition

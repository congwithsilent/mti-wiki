# 211应用文写作练习：商务贺信（2026年真题）

## 题目要求

根据以下情境与要求，撰写一篇实用、规范的英语应用文：

> **情境**：以公司的名义，给贵司的长期商业合作伙伴写一封**贺信（Congratulatory Letter）**，庆祝对方公司成立/开业 20 周年。  
> **要求**：  
> 1. 词数不少于 100 词（建议 100-120 词）；  
> 2. 包含称谓、致贺事由、回顾合作与肯定成就、展望未来合作、祝愿语、落款等规范书信要素；  
> 3. 语言正式、得体，突出专业性与商业礼节。

---

## 作答区域

Congratulatory Letter

Dear Mr.Li,
	On behalf of my company, I'm writing to extend our warmest congratulation on the 20th anniversary of your company.
	Over the past two decades, your company has achieve remarkble success and established a solid reputation through dedication and innovation. We feel truly honored to have been your long-term partner, witnessing your extraordinary growth.
	We look forward to deepen our cooperation and achieve more mutual benefits in the years ahead. May your company continue to prosper and reach new breakthrough in the future.
Sincerely yours,
Li Hua

## 一、 练习原文

这个命题，牵涉到许多古典学者、专家的思想转变。有个网友对笔者注释的《老子》，嘲讽为“开着奥迪进孔庙”。因为在许多古典学者的眼里，出口“子曰诗云”，闭口“之乎者也”，才有谈论古典的资格，才显示博古、训诂。

我们分析一下前些年，于丹的“心得”为何一下火爆，其中一点就是用现代语言谈《论语》。尽管她是借《论语》说事，尽管她解说的与《论语》驴唇不对马嘴，尽管她在某些地方曲解了汉字的本意，甚至胡言乱语，但她却迎合了一部分人，特别是年轻人的心理。因为国学热、国学出口迫使年轻人以民族文化点缀自己。

在现有的古典译文中，要么整篇繁考，最终不知所云；要么古僻，“之乎者也”夹生现代语言；要么文理不通，难以用正常逻辑思维去理解。这些都费力费神，现在年轻人很不愿去“消费”。年轻人需要的是，既是古典的正解，又是一看就明了的现代语言。因此，是否能与现代语言接轨，是关系到中华是否能够很好传承的大问题。

再者，我们研究古典，传承国学，并不是为研究而研究，为传承而传承，研究是为了应用，应用于现时社会，把先哲的思想用于现时的齐家、为业、治国。传承是为了弘扬，弘扬民族精华，摈弃其糟粕，使中华民族在精神上永不颓废，否则就失去意义。

## 三、 我的译文（初稿录入区）

This preposition is related to the shift in thinking among scholars and experts studying ancient classic works. An internet user dismissed my interpretion of Tao Te Ching as "driving an Audi into the Temple of Confucius". Because, in most of scholars' view, only those who cite scriptures and use abstruse words are qualified to discuss the classic works and demonstrate their knowledge about ancient texts and ancient words.

If we analyse the soaring popularity of Yu Dan's interpretion of the Analects, One factor is using the modern language. Although she puts forward her view on ohter things, although her interpretion could be hardly more wrong, and although she distorted the original meaning of text and even talks nonsense, she caters to some groups, especially the young people. The ongoing popularity of ancient culture and its exports, young people have to draw on their national culture as a cosmetic exercise.

Among these modern version of ancient classics, some of them are burdened with expressing research, resulting in incoherence; Some of them are abstruse, blending ancient language with modern language; And some of them are illogical, making it hard to comprehend in modern thinking. All of these are both physically and intellcetually demanding, leaving the young people have little willing to read it. What they need is a version that has accurate explanation and plain and clear language that easy to read. Therefore, *the extent of which the modern version of ancient texts can be aligned with modern language decides whether the culture can be effectively passed down or not.*

Moreover, we research the classic texts and pass down the ancient culture is not only for the sake of researching and passing down. Research should aims to apply them to modern society, using ancient wisdom to guide the family life, career development, and national governance. In passing them down, we should aim to keep alive what works and discard what doesn't *and to ensure that our nation remains spiritually strong.* Otherwise, all our efforts would be meaningless.

### 错误清单（按严重度）

**A. 词义混淆/反转（最危险）**
| 原文 | 你的译文 | 问题诊断与正确表达 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 这个**命题** | This **preposition** | **极高危形近词错位**：`preposition` 是语法中的“介词”！命题、论题应为 `proposition / thesis / issue`。 |
| 国学**出口** | its **exports** | **语域严重错位**：`exports` 是商业进出口贸易货品！文化走出去、向外传播应为 `its promotion abroad / global outreach / spread around the world`。 |
| 借《论语》**说事** | puts forward her view on **ohter things** | **理解偏差 + 拼写错误**：不是“在其他事情上提出观点”，而是“以《论语》为幌子表达自己的看法”，应为 `puts forward her own views while explaining the classic / uses the text as a vehicle to air her ideas`。 |
| 整篇**繁考** | burdened with **expressing research** | **Chinglish 硬套**：“繁考”是繁琐、堆砌考据引证，应为 `pedantic textual criticism / tedious textual research`。 |

**B. 语法硬伤（历史重复问题密集爆发）**
1. **双重谓语/主语残缺（句子结构崩塌）**：
   - 原文：“我们研究古典，传承国学，并不是为研究而研究……”
   - 译文：`we research the classic texts and pass down the ancient culture is not only for the sake of...`
   - **诊断**：一个完整的主谓句 `we research ... and pass down ...` 直接硬生生挂在系动词 `is` 前面当主语！这是考场大忌。
   - **正规表达**：必须动名词化或名词化：`Studying classic texts and preserving traditional culture is not done merely for its own sake.` 或 `Our study of ancient classics is not an end in itself.`。
2. **悬空名词短语（缺少引导词/连词）**：
   - 原文：“因为国学热、国学出口迫使年轻人以民族文化点缀自己。”
   - 译文：`The ongoing popularity of ancient culture and its exports, young people have to draw on...`
   - **诊断**：前面是一整个孤立的名词短语，打了个逗号后直接另起主谓句，缺少连词或介词，沦为悬空碎片。
   - **正规表达**：`Driven by the craze for traditional culture and its global reach, young people...` 或 `With traditional culture booming at home and spreading abroad, young people...`。
3. **情态动词接第三人称单数（历史第 6 次复发·红色警报）**：
   - `Research should aims to apply them...` $\rightarrow$ `should` 后面居然加了单三 `aims`！（与 09-17 的 `will says` 完全同源！必须强制为动词原形 `should aim to`）。
4. **限定词与单复数破裂（历史第 6 次复发）**：
   - `Among these modern version...` $\rightarrow$ `these` 后面必须是复数 `these modern versions`！
5. **从句缺系动词/谓语（连续第 4 练复发·红色警报）**：
   - `plain and clear language that easy to read` $\rightarrow$ 定语从句 `that` 修饰 language，后面直接接形容词 `easy`，**又漏掉了系动词 is**！必须是 `that is easy to read`！
6. **使役动词/非谓语接原形动词**：
   - `leaving the young people have little willing to read it` $\rightarrow$ `leave` 不能接动词原形 `have`；`willing` 是形容词，不是不可数名词；代词 `it` 与前面的 versions 单复数矛盾。应为：`leaving young people with little desire to read them` 或 `leaving young people unwilling to read them`。
7. **定语从句介词搭配错误（偷看斜体部分）**：
   - `the extent of which...` $\rightarrow$ 固定搭配是 `to the extent`，定语从句介词提前必须是 `the extent to which...`，绝不可用 `of which`！
8. **时态在并列从句中大幅漂移**：
   - `Although she puts forward... although her interpretion could be hardly more wrong... and although she distorted... and even talks...` $\rightarrow$ 在同一层的三个 although 从句里，从现在时（puts）跳到情态（could），再跳到过去式（distorted），再跳回现在时（talks），时态基准失控。统一为一般现在时：`distorts... talks...`。

**C. 用词与细节瑕疵**
1. **高频词连续 3 次拼写错误**：
   - 全文 3 次出现 `interpretion`，全部漏掉了字母 `a`！必须刻进肌肉记忆：`in-ter-pre-ta-tion`。
2. **副词语序错位**：
   - `could be hardly more wrong` $\rightarrow$ 副词 `hardly` 应位于助动词之后、连系动词之前，即 `could hardly be more wrong`。
3. **分号后无故大写**：
   - 分号（;）后不是新句，`Some of them...` 与 `And some of them...` 中的首字母严禁大写。

### 斜体偷看部分还原诊断与考场无提示方案

你标注的偷看部分：
1. `*the extent of which the modern version of ancient texts can be aligned with modern language decides whether the culture can be effectively passed down or not.*`
   * **考场脱稿备用解法**：考场如果想不起 `to what extent / the extent to which` 这种复杂的主语从句，**果断拆句用条件句或 whether 主语从句**：
     * `Whether these classics can align with modern language holds the key to the effective transmission of Chinese civilization.`
     * 既避开了 `the extent to which` 介词定从出错的风险，又把“关系到……的大问题”化为 `holds the key to / is vital to`，更轻快且不容易扣分。
2. `*and to ensure that our nation remains spiritually strong.*`
   * **考场脱稿备用解法**：考场如果卡在“精神上永不颓废”，直接抓反义核心词：
     * “颓废”的反义是“保持活力/生机”：`keep our national spirit vibrant` 或 `invigorate our national spirit`。

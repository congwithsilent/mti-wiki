## 政论（Political Discourse）
`[7句]`

> 来源标注：`[模仿]` = 用户造句修正版；`[官方]` = 官方原文句

1. After the 19th National Congress of the CPC, socialism with Chinese characteristics entered a new era. `[模仿]`
   - 修正点：`of the CPC`（需定冠词）；`a new era`（不是 an）

2. We should develop new-quality productive forces to drive innovation-led growth. `[模仿]`
   - 修正点：`productive forces`（固定复数，无单数形式）

3. Socialism with Chinese characteristics for a new era charts the course for China's development in the 21st century. `[模仿]`
   - 术语核心：`for a new era` 固定嵌入专名，介词不可换

4. We should satisfy the people's ever-growing needs for cultural consumption. `[模仿]`
   - 修正点：`needs`（复数）；`for`（need for sth.）；`consumption`（拼写）

5. The Party announced a new philosophy of development centered on the people. `[模仿]`
   - 术语核心：`philosophy of development`（不是 developing philosophy）

6. China and the US are the two biggest economic partners in global trade. `[模仿]`
   - 修正点：`partners`（复数）；区别：economic = 经济的 / economical = 节俭的

7. Enhancing the wellbeing of the people is the fundamental goal of China's development. `[模仿]`
   - 术语核心：`wellbeing`（增进民生福祉的核心词）

## 卡壳词本（概要/写作主动词汇）
> 来源：211 概要练习批改。练习中想不起来的主动词汇，每周日过一遍。

### 2026-09-07 批次（材料 001 · AI 与以人为本）
- **主体性**：human agency / human subjectivity（不是 autonomy——autonomy 偏"自主权"）
- **以人为本**：people-centered / putting people first（不是 human-based）
- **服务于人**：serve people / in the service of humanity
- **伦理的 / 伦理**：ethical / ethics（拼写：不是 ethnical）
- **协作者**：collaborator / partner（copilot 可用但偏产品名）
- **被动响应的工具**：a passive tool that merely responds（responsive ≠ 被动）
- **过度索权**：excessive permission demands
- **知情、质疑、中止**：stay informed, question, and intervene
- **隐蔽侵蚀**：covert erosion
- **本末倒置**：put the cart before the horse / an inversion of ends and means
- **人工在场**：mandatory human presence / human-in-the-loop
- **人类终审**：final human review
- **责任到人**：individual accountability / responsibility assigned to individuals
- **制度设计与伦理约束**：institutional design and ethical constraints
- **分工清晰、协同高效、权责明晰**：clear division of labor, efficient coordination, well-defined accountability
- **在此背景下**：against this backdrop（不是 under this situation）

### 2026-09-09 批次（357 练习 ·《谈抄袭》文学材料 ★5）
- **抄袭（名/动）**：plagiarism / plagiarized（**拼写：pla-gia-，不是 pla-gri-，本篇错 3 次**）
- **借鉴**：a (legitimate) borrowing of ideas / draw inspiration（**不是 reference**，reference = 参考/引用）
- **眼神**：the look in his eyes / his eyes（**eyesight = 视力，高危混淆**）
- **看出来 / 识破**：detect / see through（figure out = 弄明白，不接"识破"义）
- **春风满面**：be all smiles / radiant with joy（不是 confident——先拆义再选词）
- **踌躇满志**：enormously proud / full of pride and satisfaction（ambitious = 有野心，偏贬）
- **大肚便便**：big-bellied（fat 贬义过强且笼统）
- **（画作的）一笔/笔触**：brushstroke / stroke（不是 touch）
- **一看**：at a glance / at first sight（不是 put the sight on）
- **疲惫地**：exhausted（定语）/ wearily（状语）（不是 with tiredness）
- **不够格**：not qualified (for) / doesn't deserve（incapable 指人无能力）
- **进进出出的人**：people entering and leaving
- **都市一隅**：a corner of a city
- **散场离去的人**：audience leaving after the show
- **再也（不）**：never again / no longer
- **可惜（转折）**：It's a pity, but... / In fact（单独 however 只剩转折丢了惋惜）
- **很到位/逼真**：realistically reproduced / true to life / vividly portrayed
- **创作奖**：awards for excellence in art production / a prize of creation
- **with 复合结构**：with + n. + doing/done/adj，**内部绝不能再出现谓语动词**

### 2026-09-08 批次（357 练习 · 哈工大 2025 美好与悲伤）
- **来源于**：derive from / arise from（**deprive = 剥夺，形近义反，高危**）
- **精神的愉悦**：mental pleasure（delightment 非常用词）
- **一念之间**：a single thought / a mere thought
- **取决于**：depend on / be determined by
- **人造的美**：man-made beauty（artificial 含"虚假"贬义）
- **自然的景观**：natural landscape / scenery
- **亲情**：family ties
- **美术**：fine arts
- **说到底**：fundamentally / ultimately（after all = 毕竟）
- **得不到这样，可以得到那样**：if one is out of reach, another is always available
- **沉浸在思念之中**：be lost in memories of / lose oneself in memories of
- **能否**：whether one can (or not)（点题句的悬置结构，不可译成肯定式）

### 2026-09-11 批次（357 练习 · 川外 2026 统考《围棋如人生》★5）
- **围棋 / 棋子**：Go（棋类无冠词，play Go）/ stones 或 playing pieces（**chess = 国际象棋，本篇错 2 次**）
- **朝鲜半岛**：the Korean Peninsula（Korea 单用指向不明）
- **东北亚**：Northeast Asia（专名大写，无 -ern）
- **先后传到**：was introduced first to...and then to / successively
- **为……普遍喜爱**：widely popular in / a favorite among
- **文化交流的工具**：an instrument of cultural exchange（way of cultural communication 偏中式）
- **琴棋书画**：qin (the guqin), qi (Go), shu (calligraphy), hua (painting) — the four arts of the Chinese scholar
- **文人**：scholars / men of letters（intellectuals 偏现代政治语境）
- **案台上常备**：could be found on the desks of scholars / kept at hand
- **待客（有酒有棋）**：entertain guests with liquor and the game（treatment ≠ 招待；wine 特指葡萄酒，中式待客 liquor）
- **对弈**：pit their wits against each other
- **观棋的人 / 围观**：spectators / onlookers；「旁边站着一大片」surrounded by
- **丝毫不比……少**：no less...than（**≠ even more than，「不少于」≠「更多」，本篇 A 级反转**）
- **注入（体现）**：be an embodiment of / embody（inject = 注射）
- **用棋子说人生**：life is often compared to the stones（「说」= 打比方 compare，非 express）
- **激昂飞动而妙趣横生**：never ceases to excite much interest and delight / exciting and delightful
- **天地**：a world（space = 物理空间/太空）
- **茶香至日夕，围棋自穷年**：The fragrance of tea lingers from dawn till dusk, while the game of Go fills the passing time in quiet leisure（**两句并列赞美用 while/and，禁 but 转折**）

### 2026-09-15 批次（211 命题作文 · 传统文化的传承与创新）
- **传承文化**：pass down / pass on cultural heritage / inherit and carry forward（**不是 pass through**，pass through = 经过/穿过，高危连错）
- **归属感**：a sense of belonging（**belongings = 个人行李/随身财物**）
- **作为……基石/里程碑**：serve as a milestone / stand as the cornerstone（**不是 remark as**）
- **以……为特征**：be characterized by / marked by（**不是 featured with**）
- **坚守根基**：adhere to our roots（**adhere 必接 to**）
- **合情合理**：make sense（**不是 have more sense**）
- **化解/弥合代沟**：bridge the temporal divide
- **不仅是……而是……**：not an obsolete relic, but a living spring
- **抵御文化同质化**：resist the rising tide of cultural homogenization
- **精神锚点**：an indispensable spiritual anchor

### 2026-09-16 批次（211 概要 · 材料 002 营商环境与统一大市场）
- **市场预期**：market expectations（**不是 prediction**，prediction = 预测/预报）
- **市场准入**：market access（**没有 approvement 这个词**）
- **财政补贴/政策优惠**：fiscal subsidies / policy incentives（**不是 bonus**，bonus = 员工分红/奖金）
- **重复建设**：redundant construction / duplicate industrial projects（**不是 repeated construction**）
- **招商引资**：investment promotion / attract capital and businesses（**不是 irregular attraction**）
- **制度障碍/壁垒**：institutional barriers / market entry obstacles（**不是 systemic restriction**）
- **提振市场信心**：shore up / boost market confidence（**不是 encourage the market**）
- **公平竞争的环境**：a level playing field / a fair and competitive market
- **过度政策竞争/内卷**：excessive competition in policy perks / a race to the bottom
- **过去常常依赖……**：used to rely on...（**严禁写 are used to reduce，那成了被动语态**）

### 2026-09-17 批次 1（357 汉译英 · 广西科大 2025 低空经济与无人机）
- **型号合格证**：type certificate（**航空制造业固定专名，不是 model qualification**）
- **使人联想起……**：reminiscent of... / remind sb. of...（**remind 搭配 of，绝非 with**）
- **大规模生产**：large-scale production / mass production（**不宜写作 massive production**）
- **空域管理**：airspace management（**不是 regulation of airspace**）
- **监管批准**：regulatory approval（**不可数，不是 permissions**）
- **寻求……批准**：seek type approval（**及物动词 seek 直接接宾语，不加 for**）
- **领跑优势/技术先发优势**：leading edge / competitive advantage（**technical advantage on EV 偏中式**）
- **科幻小说（体裁）**：science fiction（**不可数，不加 s**）
- **标志着……**：meaning that... / marking... / signifying...（**remark 是口头评论，不能指“标志着”**）
- **培育增长新引擎**：foster / cultivate new engines of economic growth（**mention 后接 doing 或从句，不接 to do**）

### 2026-09-17 批次 2（357 汉译英 · 文学散文《善的第一反应》）
- **段子（蕴含哲理的故事/轶事）**：story / anecdote（**绝不能写 humor**，humor = 幽默/诙谐，会彻底曲解文章基调）
- **山景/风景**：scenery / landscape / mountain view（**绝不能写 scenario**，scenario = 方案/设想情景/剧本）
- **五花八门/各色各样**：varied / diverse / a variety of（**variable = 易变的/变量，词性与词义双重错位**）
- **按常理**：typically / usually / as a rule / normally（**common sense = 常识**）
- **卖关子/留悬念**：keep sb. in suspense / leave sb. in suspense（**不可写 leave us a suspense**）
- **读到……新闻报道**：read news / come across reports（**英语中 news/reports 严禁搭配 meet**）
- **劫后余生/死里逃生**：escape a close brush with death / narrow escape from disaster（**不是 feeling of survival**）
- **他人的利益**：others' interests（**他人为复数代词 others，所有格为 others'**）
- **与过去相反的虚拟语气公式**：If + had done..., S + would/could/might have done...（**核心语法铁律，禁写 if didn't... can do**）
- **落入深谷**：plunge into a deep valley / fall into a chasm（**动作链切勿漏译**）
- **巨石**：boulder / huge rock
- **惊魂甫定**：reeling from the shock / when they calmed down
- **不无感慨地**：feelingly / emotionally / with a heavy heart
- **停车靠边**：pull over / call for a halt
- **拐弯（车辆）**：round a bend / make a turn
- **系于一念**：be determined by a flashing thought across one's mind




### 2026-09-23 批次（357 汉译英 · 南大 2023 MA 真题重译）
- **命题/议题**：proposition / thesis（**严禁误写为介词 preposition，形近大错**）
- **解读/阐释**：interpretation（**拼写核验：inter-pre-ta-tion，含 a，切勿漏拼为 interpretion**）
- **借题发挥/借……说事**：put forward one's own views while explaining the text / use sth. as a vehicle to air one's opinions（**不是 puts forward view on other things**）
- **文化走出去/国学出口**：global outreach of traditional culture / its spread around the world（**严禁使用商业货品出口 exports**）
- **装点门面/点缀自己**：a cosmetic exercise / a decorative veneer
- **繁考（繁琐考据）**：pedantic textual criticism / tedious textual research（**严禁字面硬拼 expressing research**）
- **费力费神**：mentally and intellectually demanding / exhausting（**拼写：in-tel-lec-tual-ly，注意 c 与 t 的相对位置**）
- **毫无阅读意愿**：have little desire/willingness to read / be unwilling to read（**willing 是形容词，名词是 willingness，绝不能写 have little willing**）
- **一看就明了的现代语言**：plain, accessible modern language
- **与……接轨**：be aligned with / bridge the gap with
- **在……程度上（定从搭配）**：the extent to which...（**固定搭配：to the extent，定语从句必须用 to which，绝非 of which**）
- **齐家、为业、治国**：managing family affairs, pursuing a career, and governing a nation
- **取其精华，去其糟粕**：keep alive what works and discard what doesn't / preserve the essence and discard the dross
- **精神上永不颓废**：remain spiritually strong / stay spiritually robust
- **句型结构铁律**：studying... is not merely...（动名词作主语，**严禁使用 we research... is not... 双重谓语**）
- **情态动词铁律**：情态动词后强制跟动词原形（**绝不能写 should aims / will says，连续第 6 次警报**）

# 211命题作文写作练习：传统文化的传承与创新

## 题目要求

> **Topic**:  
> In an era marked by rapid globalization and technological advancement, some argue that traditional culture is losing its relevance and should give way to modern values, while others believe that preserving and revitalizing cultural heritage is more crucial than ever.  
> **Write an argumentative essay of no less than 350 words** to discuss this issue. State your position clearly and support your arguments with sound reasoning and relevant examples.

---

### 推荐论证骨架与四段式逻辑（建议 360 - 400 词）

1. **Para 1 引论（~60 词）· 破题与立意**：
   - 展现全球化与现代化对本土传统文化的冲击；
   - 确立核心论点：**传承绝非固步自封的封存，而是兼顾“守正”与“创新”的动态新生**（Preservation does not imply stagnation; genuine heritage protection lies in dynamic innovation and modern integration）。
2. **Para 2 传承之“根”（~120 词）· 文化认同与精神支柱**：
   - 传统文化是民族精神的根脉（cultural anchor）与国家认同（national identity）的基石；
   - 在全球同质化（homogenization）背景下，独特文化基因是多元文明不可或缺的瑰宝。
3. **Para 3 创新之“道”（~120 词）· 现代融合与技术赋能**：
   - 传统不能沦为博物馆里的冰冷化石，必须与现代生活相结合（例如：国潮文创、数字化重现如数字敦煌/故宫、新媒体传播）；
   - 赋予传统文化以现代表达形式，才能激活其当代生命力。
4. **Para 4 结论（~70 词）· 总结升华**：
   - 总结：坚守根脉（adhere to roots）与开拓创新（break new ground）相辅相成；
   - 唯有在守正中创新，传统文化方能历久弥新，为人类文明贡献独特的东方智慧。

---

### 高阶表达与搭配储备
- `cultural anchor / bedrock of national identity`（精神锚点 / 民族认同基石）
- `resist the rising tide of cultural homogenization`（抵御文化同质化浪潮）
- `not an obsolete relic, but a living spring`（不是过时的遗迹，而是流动的活水）
- `bridge the temporal divide`（弥合时代代沟）
- `digital preservation and creative adaptation`（数字化保护与创造性转化）
- `strike a harmonious balance between continuity and innovation`（在传承与创新中寻求和谐统一）

## 作答区域

To Be or Not To Be: How should we consider traditional culture nowadays?


Now we are living in an era featured with rapid globalization and technological advancement, which brings changing moral values and opinions. Some argue that the traditional culture is losing its relevance, and adopting them to embrace the moderm values is the right path; Others think that our priority now is to preserve and revitalize those cultural heritage. In my opinion, It is not a black-or-white issue, and to transform the traditional culture into new modern form may have more sense.
Traditional culture, and its cultural heritage, remarks as the milestone of a nation. It embedded the glory, the misery, the shame and even the hope of a nation, playing a significant role as mental power when pursuing their prosperity and strong. Fully adopting the traditional culture will results in the lack of belongings, which can cause the seperation of a time-honored nation.
However, those artifacts in the museum can hardly attract young people, who is the mainstrean for passing through the culture. To attract their attention, transforming the cultural heritage into modern form is crucial. For example, translating those obscure old classics into modern language and perform it in the way of social media can allow more young people to feel the beauty of ancient wisdom, so they can pay more attention on the traditional culture and pass through them.
Protecting traditional culture is a comprehensive project. To strike a harmonious balance between continuity and innovation, we should break new ground while adhering the roots, so that we can pass through our culture and contribute the unique eastern wisdom to the world.

#### 1. A 级：概念误用与语义反转（最高扣分项）

| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Para 3 & 4 | `passing through the culture` <br> `pass through them` <br> `pass through our culture` | **短语误用（全篇连错 3 次）**。`pass through` 是“穿过、途经”（如 pass through the gate），绝无“传承”之意。                                             | **`pass down / pass on the culture`** <br> 或 `inherit and carry forward cultural heritage`               |
| Para 2     | `lack of belongings`                                                                   | **词义混淆**。`belongings`（复数）专指“个人随身财物、行李”；“归属感”是抽象名词不可数。                                                                     | **`a sense of belonging`**                                                                               |

#### 2. B 级：语法与搭配硬伤
- **主谓一致错误**：
  - Para 3：`young people, who is the mainstrean` -> `young people` 是复数人称，定语从句必须用 **`who are`**；且 `mainstrean` 拼写错误（正确为 **`mainstream`**）。
- **介词搭配缺失/误用**：
  - Para 4：`while adhering the roots` -> `adhere` 是不及物动词，必须接介词 `to`，应为 **`while adhering to our roots`**。
  - Para 3：`pay more attention on` -> 固定搭配应为 **`pay more attention to`**。
- **平行结构失调**：
  - Para 3：`translating those obscure old classics into modern language and perform it...` -> `translating` 与 `perform` 并列，应统一使用动名词 **`and performing / adapting them`**；且前面的代词是复数 `classics`，此处代词不能用单数 `it`，必须用 **`them`**。
  - Para 2：`prosperity and strong` -> 名词与形容词并列错误，应改为同为名词的 **`prosperity and strength`**。
- **标点与大小写**：
  - Para 1：`is the right path; Others think...` -> 分号后首字母应小写（`; others think`）。
  - Para 1：`In my opinion, It is not...` -> 逗号后首字母应小写（`, it is not`）。
  - Para 4：`unique eastern wisdom` -> “东方智慧”专有名词需大写（`Eastern wisdom`）。

### 四、高分扩写与对照示范（386 词 · 考场可直接套用）

> **Approaching Traditional Culture in the Modern Era: A Dynamic Balance**
> 
> We are currently living in an era **characterized by** rapid globalization and technological advancement, which has brought about profound shifts in cultural values and social perspectives. Some argue that traditional culture is gradually losing its contemporary relevance, suggesting that abandoning outdated norms to embrace modern values is the only pragmatic path. Others, however, insist that safeguarding cultural heritage should remain our paramount priority. In my perspective, this is not a black-or-white dilemma; rather, **transforming cultural heritage into dynamic modern forms makes far more sense than outright rejection or rigid preservation.**
> 
> To begin with, traditional culture **stands as the cornerstone of national identity** and collective memory. It encapsulates the historical triumphs, tribulations, and enduring aspirations of a civilization, serving as an indispensable spiritual anchor. In an age where global cultural homogenization threatens local distinctiveness, a deep connection to one's ancestral roots provides citizens with a coherent **sense of belonging** and psychological stability. Conversely, completely severing ties with cultural traditions would alienate individuals from their historical roots, ultimately weakening the cohesion of a time-honored nation.
> 
> Nevertheless, cultural preservation does not mean merely encasing antique relics in museum display cases, which often fails to resonate with the younger generation—**the very demographic entrusted with passing down this heritage**. To bridge this temporal divide, creative innovation is imperative. For instance, translating abstruse classical texts into accessible vernacular narratives and presenting them via digital platforms or immersive media can effectively demystify ancient wisdom. Moreover, integrating traditional motifs into contemporary fashion, digital gaming, and creative industries—a phenomenon vividly illustrated by the recent "China-chic" wave—proves that tradition can thrive as a vibrant living culture rather than an obsolete souvenir.
> 
> In conclusion, revitalizing traditional culture is a systemic and delicate undertaking. To strike a harmonious balance between continuity and innovation, we must remain firmly anchored in our cultural roots while daring to break new ground. Only through such dynamic adaptation can we perpetuate our historical legacy and continue to contribute unique Eastern wisdom to human civilization.

2. **牢记抽象名词形式**：“归属感”是 `a sense of belonging`（不可数），`belongings` 是随身行李；

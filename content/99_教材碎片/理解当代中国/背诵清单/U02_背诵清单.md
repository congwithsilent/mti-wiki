## 二、典范例句

### （一）语义对应

#### 例句 1

> **【汉语原文】** 坚定不移"打虎"、"拍蝇"、"猎狐"。
>
> **【参考译文】** We have taken firm action to "take out tigers," "swat flies," and "hunt down foxes".

<!-- 来源：学生用书U2「语义聚焦·1.1语义对应」例1 -->

#### 例句 2

> **【汉语原文】** 巡视利剑作用彰显，实现中央和省级党委巡视全覆盖。
>
> **【参考译文】** Disciplinary inspections have cut like a blade through corruption and misconduct; they have covered every Party committee in all departments at the central and provincial levels.

<!-- 来源：学生用书U2「语义聚焦·1.1语义对应」例2 -->

#### 例句 3

> **【汉语原文】** 坚持照镜子、正衣冠、洗洗澡、治治病的要求，开展党的群众路线教育实践活动和"三严三实"专题教育。
>
> **【参考译文】** We have committed to "examining ourselves in the mirror, tidying our attire, taking a bath, and treating our ailments," launched activities to see members command and act on the Party's mass line, and initiated a campaign for the observance of the Three Guidelines for Ethical Behavior and Three Basic Rules of Conduct.

<!-- 来源：学生用书U2「语义聚焦·1.1语义对应」例3 -->

#### 例句 4

> **【汉语原文】** 我反复讲，鞋子合不合脚，只有穿的人才知道。中国特色社会主义制度好不好、优越不优越，中国人民最清楚，也最有发言权。
>
> **【参考译文】** I have often said that only the wearer of the shoes knows if they fit or not. The Chinese people know best whether the Chinese socialist system suits the country or not.

<!-- 来源：学生用书U2「语义聚焦·1.1语义对应」例4 -->

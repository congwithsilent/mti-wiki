# Unit 05 坚持以人民为中心的发展思想

## 一、核心概念与时政术语速查 (Key Concepts)

| 序号 | 中文核心概念 / 术语 | 官方标准译文 (Official Translation) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 以人民为中心 | people-centered |
| 2 | 全过程人民民主 | whole-process people’s democracy |
| 3 | 全体人民共同富裕 | common prosperity for all/for everyone/for all our people |
| 4 | 坚持房子是用来住的、不是用来炒的定位。 | We must not forget that housing is for living in, not for speculation. |
| 5 | 我将无我，不负人民。 | I will fully commit to the people and never fail them. |
| 6 | 天不言而四时行，地不语而百物生。 | Heaven and earth do not speak, yet the seasons change and all things grow. |
